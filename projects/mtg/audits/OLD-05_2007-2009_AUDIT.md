# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2007–2009 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**FUT · 10E · ME1 · LRW · MOR · SHM · EVE · ME2 · ALA · CFX · ARB · M10 · FVE · HOP · ME3 · PDS**
(Future Sight, 10th Edition, Masters Edition, Lorwyn, Morningtide, Shadowmoor, Eventide,
Masters Edition II, Shards of Alara, Conflux, Alara Reborn, Magic 2010, From the Vault: Exiled,
Planechase, Masters Edition III, Premium Deck Series: Slivers).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the SOS backlog. Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the
underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no supported example exists.

**Every** card in these 16 missing-lists is a **catalogued-unsupported** entry: upstream registered
it with full oracle text inside `sets/primitives/unsupported.txt` (grade *unsupported* → does not
load, not AI-deck safe), but never wrote a working primitive. None are stale (none silently already
work), and none are true dangling references outside the catalogue. So the whole population is
"known-broken, documented" — identical in shape to the 2000–2003 era.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Clash "return-to-hand" & other-clause riders  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (many, 27)

Clash itself is supported (Kithkin Zealot / Wild Ricochet). Every card here is blocked by the
*rider* attached to the clash, most commonly "If you win, return this to its owner's hand" (a
self-bounce recursion the primitives never wired up) or a second conditional effect. LRW/MOR
carry the bulk.

- Ringskipper (LRW) — clash on death, self-bounce
- Woodland Guidance (LRW) — untap all Forests on win + self-exile
- Pollen Lullaby (LRW) — fog + skip-untap on win
- Paperfin Rascal (LRW) — +1/+1 counter on win
- Spring Cleaning (LRW) — mass enchant-destroy on win
- Scattering Stroke (LRW) — counter + deferred mana refund on win
- Fistful of Force (LRW) — pump + trample on win
- Springjack Knight (LRW) — grants double strike on win
- Bog Hoodlums (LRW) — +1/+1 counter on win
- Sentry Oak (LRW) — combat-triggered clash, loses defender on win
- Whirlpool Whelm (LRW) — bounce, optional top-of-library on win
- Hoarder's Greed (LRW) — draw-two loop while winning
- Weed Strangle (LRW) — destroy + lifegain on win
- Nath's Elite (LRW) — +1/+1 counter on win
- Gilt-Leaf Ambush (LRW) — 2 tokens + deathtouch on win
- Oaken Brawler (LRW) — +1/+1 counter on win
- Lash Out (LRW) — 3 dmg to creature + to controller on win
- Broken Ambitions (LRW) — counter-unless-pay + mill-4 on win
- Adder-Staff Boggart (LRW) — +1/+1 counter on win
- Captivating Glance (LRW) — end-step clash control-swap aura
- Entangling Trap (LRW) — tap + don't-untap on win
- Sylvan Echoes (LRW) — draw whenever you clash-and-win
- Release the Ants (MOR) — 1 dmg, self-bounce on win
- Research the Deep (MOR) — draw, self-bounce on win
- Redeem the Lost (MOR) — protection grant, self-bounce on win
- Recross the Paths (MOR) — land into play, self-bounce on win
- Fire Juggler (MOR) — 4 dmg to blockers on win
- Pulling Teeth (MOR) — discard 2 vs 1 by outcome
- Revive the Fallen (MOR) — reanimate to hand, self-bounce on win
- Titan's Revenge (MOR) — X damage, self-bounce on win
- Rebellion of the Flamekin (LRW) — clash-triggered token maker (Tribal Enchantment)

### E2 — Coin-flip randomness  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No coin-flip primitive exists (confirmed: Mana Clash, Krark's Thumb, Ydwen Efreet all unsupported).

- Ydwen Efreet (ME1) — flip on block, remove from combat on loss

### E3 — "Destroy N chosen permanents at random"  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

Needs a random pick among chosen targets — no randomness primitive.

- Wild Swing (SHM) — choose three, destroy one at random
- Capricious Efreet (ME2) — upkeep: choose three, destroy one at random
- (Menacing Ogre secret-number is E14, not here)

### E4 — Planechase "Plane"-adjacent & premium reprint sets  · OUT-OF-SCOPE / see note  (0 Planes here)

HOP (Planechase) contributes **no `type=Plane` cards** to the missing-list — the two HOP entries
(Razia, Menacing Ogre) are ordinary cards blocked by damage-redirect / secret-number and are filed
under E9 / E14. FVE (From the Vault: Exiled) and PDS (Premium Deck: Slivers) are premium reprint
products; their single/pair of missing cards (Gifts Ungiven; Menacing Ogre) are reprints filed by
their real mechanic. So there is **no OUT-OF-SCOPE Plane bucket** in this era, unlike 2000–2003.

### E5 — "Most common color" census  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Requires a tie-aware global color-census the engine cannot compute as a filter.

- Call to Arms (ME3) — white pump while a chosen color is most-common on an opponent's board

### E6 — Copy-a-creature riders & mass/targeted copy  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

Base "enters as a copy" is supported (Clone, Body Double). These carry extra machinery.

- Vesuvan Doppelganger (ME1) — copy + recurring upkeep re-copy rider · MEDIUM
- Shapeshifter's Marrow (FUT) — becomes a copy of a milled creature (one-shot state change) · MEDIUM
- Shapesharer (LRW) — {2}{U}: target Shapeshifter copies target creature (activated, temp) · MEDIUM
- Mirrorweave (SHM) — *every other* creature becomes a copy of one target · ENGINE-BLOCKED (mass live-copy)
- Deceiver-style: (none further)

### E7 — Copy-a-spell (mass / "for each target")  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Single-target copy is supported (Twincast/Fork). These need copy-per-legal-target or copy-a-GY-card.

- Spellweaver Volute (FUT) — on sorcery cast, copy the enchanted instant in a graveyard
- (Radiate-style mass copy — none in this era's lists)

### E8 — Counter / lock spells by unusual condition  · MEDIUM  (3)

Not plain "counter target spell" (that's supported). Each keys on a condition or names cards.

- Nix (FUT) — counter target spell if no mana was spent to cast it · MEDIUM
- Grimoire Thief (MOR) — exile top 3 face-down, later counter all spells with those names · MEDIUM
- Delay (FUT) — counter, then suspend-3 the countered spell instead of graveyard · MEDIUM

### E9 — Prevention / damage-redirection shields  · MEDIUM  (many, 14)

Wagic's `prevent:` primitive is partial; these need per-source, deferred, choose-a-source, or
redirect-to-a-permanent shields (confirmed none of the redirect examples are supported).

- Intervention Pact (FUT) — choose-a-source prevent + lifegain + delayed upkeep tax
- Story Circle (10E) — choose-a-color repeatable prevention (the classic "circle")
- Greater Realm of Preservation (ME1) — black/red choose-a-source prevention
- Kjeldoran Royal Guard (10E, ME1) — redirect all unblocked combat damage to itself
- Forcefield (ME1) — prevent all but 1 from a chosen unblocked creature
- Purity (LRW) — prevent noncombat damage to you + gain that life
- Hostility (LRW) — spell damage to opponents becomes token-making
- Sacred Boon (ME2) — prevent 3 to a creature, convert to +0/+1 counters
- Scars of the Veteran (ME2) — prevent 7, convert to +0/+1 counters (alt cost)
- Nova Pentacle (ME3) — redirect next damage to a creature of opponent's choice
- Razia, Boros Archangel (HOP) — redirect next 3 from your creature to another creature
- Harm's Way (M10) — redirect next 2 to a target creature or player
- Immortal Coil (ALA) — damage → exile-from-GY replacement + lose-if-empty
- Divine Presence-style flat caps — (none further this era)

### E10 — Exile-and-return with noted counters ("stasis prisons")  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

"Exile a creature, note its counters/Auras, return it later exactly as it was." No framework for
storing an exiled permanent's full state and reattaching Auras. All probed examples unsupported.

- Oubliette (ME1) — ETB exile, LTB return with noted counters + Auras
- Tawnos's Coffin (ME1) — activated exile/return with noted counters + Auras
- Icy Prison (ME2) — ETB exile, sac-unless-pay upkeep, LTB return
- Colfenor's Urn (LRW) — exile dying big creatures, mass-return at end step
- Seraph (ME1) — reanimate creatures it killed, sacrifice on loss of control (state-tracked)

### E11 — Reanimate-what-I-damaged  · MEDIUM  (2)

"When a creature dealt damage by ~ dies, put it onto the battlefield under your control." Needs a
damage-source→death link plus a sacrifice-on-loss-of-control clause.

- Krovikan Vampire (ME2) — end-step reanimate of creatures it killed
- The Wretched (ME3) — gain control of everything blocking it while it lives

### E12 — Text-changing effects  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

"Replace all instances of one color/land-type word." No text-substitution layer (all unsupported).

- Mind Bend (10E) — change a color/land-type word on a permanent
- Glamerdye (EVE) — change a color word on a spell or permanent (+ retrace)
- Muraganda Petroglyphs (FUT) — "creatures with no abilities get +2/+2" (needs a live no-abilities test — same text-parsing gap)
- (Alter Reality etc. are earlier-era)

### E13 — Move / manipulate counters between permanents  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

No primitive to relocate counters from one creature to another (both probed unsupported).

- Fate Transfer (MOR) — move ALL counters from one creature to another
- Leech Bonder (MOR) — {U},{Q}: move a counter between creatures ({Q} untap symbol)

### E14 — Secret-number / hidden-choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Simultaneous secret numeric choice — no digital analog (same family as Menacing Ogre in 2000–2003).

- Menacing Ogre (HOP, PDS) — each player secretly picks a number, highest loses that much life

### E15 — Name-a-card gambles (reveal / mill / discard by name)  · MEDIUM  (6)

Naming is supported in isolation, but each needs a name-then-check-a-hidden-zone loop.

- Lammastide Weave (LRW) — name, mill target, gain life if match, draw
- Demonic Consultation (ME2) — name, exile 6, dig until named card
- Nebuchadnezzar (ME3) — name, opponent reveals X random, discard matches
- Petra Sphinx (ME1) — name, reveal top, hand-or-graveyard by match
- Thought Hemorrhage (ARB) — name nonland, reveal hand, 3 dmg per copy, exile all copies everywhere
- Sphinx Ambassador (ME2) — search library, opponent names a card, may cheat a nonmatch in

### E16 — Grandeur / multi-step bounce cascade  · MEDIUM  (1)

Grandeur is supported (Korlash, Rhys). This one's payoff is a sequenced multi-type bounce.

- Linessa, Zephyr Mage (FUT) — grandeur: bounce a creature, then artifact, then enchantment, then land

### E17 — "Block an additional creature" / block-any-number statics  · MEDIUM  (4)

Static combat-modifier "can block N additional / any number" — probed unsupported (High Ground,
Wall of Caltrops). Needs a blocker-count override in the combat engine.

- High Ground (10E) — each of your creatures blocks one additional creature
- Lairwatch Giant (LRW) — blocks one additional; first strike when blocking 2+
- Thoughtweft Trio (SHM/LRW-block) — can block any number (also has champion, which IS supported)
- (Errand of Duty's banded token → E20 banding)

### E18 — Gain control while enchanted / while tapped  · MEDIUM  (3)

"Gain control of target creature for as long as it's enchanted / this stays tapped." Control-theft
is supported (Control Magic) but the *duration-linked* variants are not.

- Rootwater Matriarch (ME1) — control while the creature is enchanted
- Seasinger (ME1) — control while you control it tapped, opponent has an Island
- Puca's Mischief (SHM) — upkeep exchange of ≤-CMC nonland permanents

### E19 — Exchange / swap control of two permanents  · MEDIUM  (2)

Two-way control exchange with a same-type constraint.

- Juxtapose (ME1) — swap highest-CMC creature, then highest-CMC artifact
- Gauntlets of Chaos (ME3) — swap a chosen permanent for an opponent's of shared type

### E20 — Banding  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (7)

Base banding has no working primitive (only "grants banding" lords resolve). Any card whose only
gap is banding is engine-blocked.

- Mesa Pegasus (ME1) — flying + banding
- Benalish Hero (ME1) — banding
- Knights of Thorn (ME1) — pro-red + banding
- Shield Bearer (ME2) — banding
- Icatian Phalanx (ME2) — banding
- Kjeldoran Skycaptain (ME2) — flying, first strike, banding
- Errand of Duty (ME2) — makes a 1/1 with banding
- Nature's Blessing (ME2) — grants banding/first strike/trample (banding branch blocks it)

### E21 — Horsemanship  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Portal Three Kingdoms evasion keyword; no primitive (probed unsupported).

- Liu Bei, Lord of Shu (ME3) — horsemanship + partner-boost

### E22 — Aura swap  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"{cost}: Exchange this Aura with an Aura in your hand." No hand↔battlefield Aura-swap primitive.

- Arcanum Wings (FUT) — aura swap {2}{U}

### E23 — Cumulative upkeep + granted-cumulative-upkeep  · MEDIUM  (1)

Cumulative upkeep itself is supported (Braid of Fire). This one *grants* an upkeep clause to a
targeted creature, which the primitive can't author.

- Musician (ME2) — has cumulative upkeep AND grants a music-counter upkeep tax to other creatures

### E24 — Hand-exile / look-at-hidden face-down utility  · MEDIUM  (3)

Face-down "exile from hand, look at it, return later, dump on loss of control" bookkeeping.

- Gustha's Scepter (ME2) — exile hand cards face down, return; dump to GY on loss of control
- Knowledge Vault (ME3) — exile library face down, mass-return to hand on sac
- Skill Borrower (ALA) — has all activated abilities of the revealed top card

### E25 — Search-into-two-piles / opponent-partitions choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

The 2000–2003 "two-pile" family — opponent divides objects, you pick a pile. No partition UI.

- Phyrexian Portal (ME2) — opponent splits top 10 into two piles; you pick
- Brilliant Ultimatum (ALA) — opponent splits exiled top 5; you play one pile free
- Covenant of Minds (ALA) — opponent chooses hand-vs-mill-and-draw-5 (binary opponent choice)

### E26 — Warp-World-class mass shuffle-and-reveal-into-play  · MEDIUM  (1)

Genesis Wave is supported, but Warp World's per-player, per-type, ordered mass reveal-into-play is
a much larger sequenced operation the primitives never captured.

- Warp World (10E, M10) — shuffle all permanents, reveal that many, put artifacts/creatures/lands then enchantments into play

### E27 — Alt-win / draw-the-game endgame counters  · MEDIUM  (2)

- Divine Intervention (ME3) — remove 2 counters over 2 upkeeps → game is a draw
- Lich's Mirror (ALA) — replace "you lose the game" with a full reset to 20 life

### E28 — Champion-block / regen-troll / other-clause creatures  · MEDIUM  (mixed)

- Thoughtweft Trio (SHM) — champion (supported) + block-any-number (E17 is the real blocker)

### E29 — Chroma "count colored mana symbols" payoffs  · MEDIUM  (4)

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of these names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — so this audit measures the *documented*
  unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set (all the cards
  Wagic *does* implement) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope here.
- The era's marquee mechanics are **overwhelmingly supported** — clash, devour, unearth, cascade,
  champion, hideaway, conspire, retrace, chroma, grandeur, fight, and cumulative upkeep all have
  supported exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is why so many cards land in BACKLOG-MEDIUM
  ("blocked by a rider") rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED. The single largest group is LRW/MOR **clash
  cards whose only real gap is the "if you win, return this to hand" self-bounce recursion**.
- Genuinely engine-blocked keywords confirmed by absence of any supported example: **banding**
  (only "grants banding" lords resolve), **horsemanship**, **coin-flip**, **most-common-color
  census**, **text-changing**, **move-counters-between-creatures**, **aura swap**, and the
  **exile-with-noted-state "prison"** family (Oubliette / Tawnos's Coffin / Icy Prison).
- HOP (Planechase) contributes **zero `type=Plane` cards** to these lists, so — unlike the
  2000–2003 promo file — there is no OUT-OF-SCOPE planar bucket here. The planar subsystem gap is
  real but simply doesn't surface in `missing_cards_by_sets/HOP.txt`.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics
  proven elsewhere in the catalogue.
