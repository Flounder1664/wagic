# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2010–2012 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**WWK · ROE · M11 · SOM · TD0 · PS11 · ME4 · MBS · KVD · NPH · CMD · M12 · ISD · DKA · VVK · AVR**
(Worldwake, Rise of the Eldrazi, Magic 2011, Scars of Mirrodin, Duel Decks Anthology, Premium
Deck Series: Graveborn, Masters Edition IV, Mirrodin Besieged, Duel Decks: Knights vs. Dragons,
New Phyrexia, Commander 2011, Magic 2012, Innistrad, Dark Ascension, Duel Decks: Venser vs.
Koth, Avacyn Restored).

Generated 2026-07-08. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the OLD-03 audit and the SOS backlog.

## Headline numbers

| Metric | Count |
|---|---|
| Distinct missing cards (deduped across all 16 sets) | **98** |
| STALE (name already resolves to `supported`/`borderline`) | **0** |
| Catalogued-unsupported (registered w/ oracle text in `unsupported.txt`) | **98** |
| Truly-absent (no entry anywhere) | **0** |

**Every** card in these 16 missing-lists is a **catalogued-unsupported** entry: upstream registered
it with full oracle text inside `sets/primitives/unsupported.txt` (grade *unsupported* → does not
load, not AI-deck safe), but never wrote a working primitive. None are stale (none silently already
work), and none are true dangling references outside the catalogue. So the whole population is
"known-broken, documented" — identical in shape to the OLD-03 era.

### Block mechanics are NOT the blockers

The task flagged three mechanic families to verify against `grade_index.json`. All three are
**fully supported** in the engine, so cards using them are absent for *other* reasons:

| Mechanic | Verdict | Evidence (implemented cards in `mtg.txt`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Transform / double-faced (ISD/DKA/AVR)** | SUPPORTED | Delver of Secrets, Huntmaster of the Fells, Kruin Outlaw, Mayor of Avabruck, Ludevic's Test Subject; Garruk Relentless (planeswalkers.txt) |
| **Infect / Proliferate / Metalcraft (SOM/MBS/NPH)** | SUPPORTED | Plague Stinger, Contagion Clasp, Steady Progress, Phyrexian Swarmlord; Galvanic Blast, Etched Champion, Dispatch |
| **Flashback / Morbid / Curses (ISD block)** | SUPPORTED | Faithless Looting, Think Twice, Burning Vengeance; Brimstone Volley, Reckless Waif; Curse of Death's Hold, Curse of Thirst |

Also confirmed supported and therefore *not* a blocker on its own: **base morph** (Zoetic Cavern,
Exalted Angel), **level-up** (Transcendent Master, Kargan Dragonlord), **devour** (Mycoloth,
Skullmulcher), **soulbond** (Wingcrafter, Trusted Forcemage), **totem armor** (the Umbra cycle),
**clone/copy-a-creature** (Clone, Phantasmal Image), **copy-a-spell** (Fork, Twincast, Reverberate),
and **clash** (Shivan Meteor). The only single DFC in these lists, **Soul Seizer** (DKA), is blocked
by its *turn-up-and-attach* rider, not by transform.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Deferred "next N damage from a source of your choice" prevent/redirect shields  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (7)

Wagic's `prevent:` primitive is partial: it does not model "the next N damage a *chosen source*
would deal, this turn, then redirect the prevented amount." Every prevent-then-deal card in the era
resolves to `unsupported.txt` (confirmed: Comeuppance, Deflecting Palm also unsupported).

- Harm's Way (KVD) — redirect next 2 to a creature/player
- Divine Deflection (AVR) — prevent next X, deal that much
- Refraction Trap (WWK) — prevent next 3, deal that much (also a Trap alt-cost)
- Vengeful Archon (M11) — {X}: prevent next X to you, deal that much to a player
- Pentagram of the Ages (ME4) — prevent all damage from one chosen source
- Jade Monolith (ME4) — redirect creature damage to you
- Gloom Surgeon (AVR) — replace own combat damage with self-mill (prevention replacement)

### E2 — Static combat-damage redirection to a permanent  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

"All damage that would be dealt to you by X is dealt to this creature instead" — a continuous
redirection replacement Wagic can't register generically.

- Martyrs of Korlis (ME4) — artifact damage → this
- Veteran Bodyguard (ME4) — unblocked-creature damage → this
- Personal Incarnation (ME4) — next 1 to this → its owner; any player may activate (also owner-gated)
- Hedron-Field Purists (ROE) — level-gated flat damage prevention (base level-up IS supported; the
  prevention rider is the blocker)

### E3 — Control a player's turn / control the caster mid-cast  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Confirmed unsupported catalogue-wide; there is no "take control of a player's decisions" hook.

- Mindslaver (SOM, ME4-style artifact) — you control target player's next turn *(listed once; the
  SOM printing is the missing one here)*
- Word of Command (ME4) — control the opponent while they play a chosen card from hand

### E4 — Counter / retarget an ability or spell on the stack  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Countering *abilities* (not spells) and retargeting the top of the stack. Confirmed unsupported
(Stifle, Voidmage Husher also unsupported).

- Azorius Guildmage (CMD, TD0) — mode: counter target activated ability
- Not of This World (ROE) — counter a spell/ability targeting your permanent (cost-reduction rider)
- Spellskite (NPH) — {PU}: change a target to Spellskite
- Willbender (TD0) — morph turn-up: change the target of a single-target spell/ability
  *(base morph is supported; the retarget payoff is the blocker — note its `autofaceup=` DSL attempt)*

### E5 — Copy-a-spell-for-each-object triggers  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Straight copy-a-spell (Fork/Twincast) IS supported, but the "whenever a spell targets only a single
Golem, copy it for each other Golem" mass-copy-with-retarget trigger is not.

- Precursor Golem (SOM)

### E6 — Imprint (exile-a-card-and-reference-it)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

Wagic has no imprint framework; each needs a stored exiled-card reference. (Confirmed: Strata Scythe,
Myr Welder unsupported; imprint *cousins* Isochron Scepter / Chrome Mox that only copy or filter
mana ARE supported, so the gap is specifically "reference the imprinted card's identity later.")

- Strata Scythe (SOM) — +1/+1 per land sharing the exiled land's name
- Myr Welder (MBS) — gains all activated abilities of cards exiled with it
- Knowledge Pool (MBS) — cast-from-exiled-pool loop (imprint-style shared exile)
- Necrotic Ooze (SOM) — gains all activated abilities of creature cards in *all* graveyards
  (dynamic ability-grant, imprint-adjacent)

### E7 — "Reveal top card each turn; play it / cast it for free"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Recurring per-upkeep / per-draw-step reveal-and-cast replacement. Cascade (one-shot) is supported;
these standing engines are not (Omen Machine, Wild Evocation both unsupported).

- Wild Evocation (M11) — each upkeep, reveal random card, play/cast it free
- Omen Machine (NPH) — replace draws with exile-top-and-cast
- Illusionary Mask (TD0) — cast a creature face-down for {X} paid to it (face-down non-morph engine)

### E8 — Ability-steal / "has all activated abilities of exiled/graveyard cards"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Dynamic grant of another card's activated abilities — related to E6 but graveyard/exile-sourced.

- Havengul Lich (DKA) — cast a creature from a GY, gain its activated abilities
- Dark Impostor (AVR) — exile creatures, gain all their activated abilities

### E9 — Control-exchange / give-permanents-away  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Swap or donate control. Confirmed unsupported (Callous Oppressor also unsupported; note Homeward
Path, a *return*-control card, IS supported — the gap is voluntary exchange/donation with upkeep
payoff).

- Zedruu the Greathearted (CMD) — donate permanents, draw/gain per owned-by-you-controlled-by-them
- Chromeshell Crab (CMD) — morph turn-up: exchange control of two creatures
- Ogre Geargrabber (SOM) — steal an Equipment until end of turn and attach it

### E10 — Copy-creature / mass-copy variants  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Base Clone/Phantasmal Image is supported; these are the harder "exile-and-become" and
"make-everything-a-copy" variants.

- The Mimeoplasm (CMD) — enter as a copy of one exiled creature + counters equal to the other's power
- Infinite Reflection (AVR) — enchanted creature's copy stamps every nontoken creature you control

### E11 — Join forces (each player pays mana)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (6)

"Starting with you, each player may pay any amount of mana" — a group-mana-auction primitive Wagic
does not have (all six unsupported).

- Minds Aglow (CMD) — each pays, everyone draws
- Collective Voyage (CMD) — each pays, everyone ramps basics
- Alliance of Arms (CMD) — each pays, everyone makes Soldiers
- Shared Trauma (CMD) — each pays, everyone self-mills
- Mana-Charged Dragon (CMD) — combat join-forces pump
- (Trade Secrets shares the "repeat as a player chooses" interactive shape — see E17)

### E12 — Clash  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

Clash IS supported as a keyword (Shivan Meteor loads), but each of these attaches a *distinct
win-rider* that needs its own conditional DSL, so upstream never finished them.

- Lash Out (CMD) — 3 damage, clash → 3 to controller
- Pollen Lullaby (CMD) — fog, clash → skip untap
- Scattering Stroke (CMD) — counter, clash → mana refund
- Whirlpool Whelm (CMD) — bounce, clash → put on top instead
- Conundrum Sphinx (CMD, M11) — attack: each player names a card, reveal top, keep-or-bottom
  (name-and-reveal gamble; clash-adjacent)

### E13 — Search-library lockdown with "any player may pay to ignore"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Continuous "players can't search" static plus an interactive pay-to-ignore rider. Base search-hate
exists as a partial (Aven Mindcensor is *borderline*), but these full lock + pay-off forms are
unsupported.

- Leonin Arbiter (SOM) — can't search; any player may pay {2} to ignore
- Stranglehold (CMD) — opponents can't search; skip extra turns
- Grafdigger's Cage (DKA) — can't ETB from GY/library; can't cast from there (static zone lock)

### E14 — "An opponent chooses / separates into piles" partition effects  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

Opponent-driven partitioning or selection. The two-pile split has no primitive (matches OLD-03 E1),
and the single-card "opponent picks one" needs an opponent-choice hook these lack.

- Sphinx of Uthuun (M12, VVK) — opponent splits top 5 into two piles
- Realms Uncharted (ROE) — search 4 lands, opponent chooses 2 to bin
- Murmurs from Beyond (CMD) — reveal 3, opponent chooses one to bin
- Distant Memories (MBS) — search, opponent may give it to you or you draw 3
- Molten Psyche (SOM) — wheel + metalcraft burn (metalcraft supported; the symmetric wheel-count
  damage is the blocker)

### E15 — Random-selection effects  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

"At random" choices with no deterministic hook.

- Scrambleverse (M12) — assign every nonland permanent to a random player
- Wild Swing (PS11) — choose 3, destroy one at random
- Tyrant of Discord (AVR) — opponent sacrifices a random permanent, repeat if nonland
- Rite of Ruin (AVR) — ordered escalating mass-sacrifice (choice-order + symmetric sac cascade)

### E16 — Cast-restriction / "spend only mana of a kind" locks  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Continuous restrictions on how spells may be cast — no rules hook (matches OLD-03 E6).

- Myr Superion (NPH) — spend only creature-produced mana to cast it
- Autumn's Veil (M11, M12) — this-turn "can't be countered by / targeted by blue-or-black" lock

### E17 — Interactive "unless / until a player chooses to stop" riders  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Loops and taxes gated on an opponent's repeated choice.

- Trade Secrets (CMD) — opponent may repeat draw-2/draw-4 as long as they like
- Scarwood Bandits (ME4) — steal artifact unless opponent pays {2}
- Norn's Annex (NPH) — creatures can't attack you unless controller pays {PW} each (Propaganda-tax
  with Phyrexian mana; attack-tax primitives exist but the {PW} choice rider is the gap)

### E18 — Dynamic / "for each" power-toughness & set-on-ETB values  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

- Sutured Ghoul (M12) — P/T = total P/T of exiled creatures (`power=* toughness=*`)
- Shapeshifter (ME4) — choose 0–7 each upkeep; P/T = N / 7−N (`toughness=7-*`)
- Voracious Dragon (KVD) — devour supported; ETB damage = twice *Goblins devoured* (needs a
  typed-devour counter the base primitive doesn't track)

### E19 — Enchantment/aura meta-grants & totem-armor granting  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

Base totem armor (Umbra cycle) and base auras are supported; these *grant* a keyword to a class of
permanents or reshape what counts as equipment/aura.

- Umbra Mystic (ROE) — your auras all gain totem armor
- Bludgeon Brawl (NPH) — every noncreature artifact becomes an Equipment with equip = its CMC
- Training Grounds (ROE) — creatures' activated abilities cost up to {2} less (floor-1 rider)
- Power Artifact (ME4) — enchanted artifact's abilities cost {2} less (floor-1 rider)

### E20 — Phylactery / conditional-indestructible & "sacrifice when no X" state  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Needs a tracked marker on a *chosen* permanent plus a state-based sacrifice trigger.

- Phylactery Lich (M11) — indestructible while a phylactery-countered artifact exists
- Descent into Madness (AVR) — escalating symmetric exile from board+hand per despair counter

### E21 — Reanimate-into-play attach / recursive aura movement  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Auras that move themselves, or return from GY attached to a *chosen* creature.

- Necrotic Plague (M11) — on death, return from GY attached to an opponent's creature (loop)
- Soul Seizer (DKA) — transform-and-attach on combat damage (transform supported; attach-on-flip is
  the rider)
- Call to the Kindred (DKA) — upkeep: free-cast a creature sharing a type with the enchanted creature

### E22 — Miscellaneous single-card oddities  · mixed

- Refraction Trap — see E1.
- Cellar Door (ISD) — bottom-of-library mill + conditional token · BACKLOG-EASY
- Echo Circlet (SOM) — "can block an additional creature" · BACKLOG-EASY

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of these names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — so this audit measures the *documented*
  unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The many WWK/ROE/SOM/ISD cards Wagic
  *does* implement never appear in `missing_cards_by_sets` and are out of scope here. (WWK and ROE
  in particular have only 2 and 6 missing entries respectively — near-complete sets.)
- The task's flagged mechanics were all verified supported via `grade_index.json` probes:
  **transform/DFC**, **infect/proliferate/metalcraft**, and **flashback/morbid/curses**. No card in
  these lists is engine-blocked *by* those mechanics; where such a card appears (Soul Seizer,
  Molten Psyche, Divine Reckoning, Voracious Dragon), a secondary clause is the blocker and it is
  tiered MEDIUM, not ENGINE-BLOCKED.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics proven
  elsewhere in the catalogue.
- The CMD (Commander 2011) file dominates the era's exclusions (19 of 98). Its two signature
  blockers — **join forces** (group mana auction, ENGINE-BLOCKED) and **clash win-riders**
  (MEDIUM) — account for most of it; the remainder are multiplayer control-exchange cards.
- Cumulative upkeep (Jotun Grunt, Naked Singularity) is a legacy mechanic with no current primitive;
  it surfaces only on these two ME4/TD0/CMD reprints and is treated as MEDIUM.
- No ANTE, no Planechase Plane cards, no Alchemy/digital-only rebalances, and no pure
  alternate-art variants appear in these lists — hence **zero OUT-OF-SCOPE** this era.
