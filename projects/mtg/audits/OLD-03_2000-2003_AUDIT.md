# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2000–2003 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**PCY · BTD · INV · PLS · 7ED · APC · ODY · DM · TOR · JUD · PRM · ONS · LGN · SCG · 8ED · MRD**
(Prophecy, Beatdown, Invasion, Planeshift, 7th Edition, Apocalypse, Odyssey, Duel Decks/starter,
Torment, Judgment, promos, Onslaught, Legions, Scourge, 8th Edition, Mirrodin).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the SOS backlog.

load, not AI-deck safe), but never wrote a working primitive. None are stale (none silently already
work), and none are true dangling references outside the catalogue. So the whole population is
"known-broken, documented" — the cleanest possible state for a backlog.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Two-pile / "separate into piles" choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (7)

Wagic has no primitive for "divide objects into two named piles and let a player pick a pile."
Needs an interactive multi-object partition + opponent-choice UI that does not exist.

- Do or Die (INV) — split creatures, destroy one pile
- Death or Glory (INV) — split graveyard creatures, reanimate one pile
- Fight or Flight (INV) — split attackers, only one pile may attack
- Stand or Fall (INV) — split blockers, only one pile may block
- Bend or Break (INV) — each player splits lands; opponent picks; destroy/tap
- Fortune's Favor (PRM) — opponent splits top 4 into two piles, you pick
- Dimensional Breach (SCG) — mass-exile then staged per-upkeep return (pile-like sequencing)

### E2 — Coin-flip / dice-driven loops  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (8)

Wagic has no coin-flip or repeat-until-fail primitive.

- Mana Clash (7ED, 8ED) — flip-both-until-heads loop
- Squee's Revenge (APC) — choose N, flip N times, escalating draw
- Crazed Firecat (TOR) — flip until loss, +1/+1 per win
- Impulsive Maneuvers (ODY) — per-attacker flip, double or prevent damage
- Goblin Psychopath (SCG) — flip on attack/block, redirect damage to you
- Fiery Gambit (MRD) — cascading multi-flip payoff ladder
- Krark's Thumb (MRD) — replacement: flip two coins ignore one
- Risky Move (ONS) — rotating control + flip to lose a creature

### E3 — Manual-dexterity / "hidden item" / secret-number  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

Physical-object or simultaneous-secret-reveal mechanics with no digital analog.

- Goblin Game (PLS) — hide items, simultaneous reveal
- Menacing Ogre (ONS) — each player secretly chooses a number
- Liar's Pendulum (MRD) — opponent guesses a named card in hand (bluff)

### E4 — Planechase "Plane" cards  · OUT-OF-SCOPE  (6)

`type=Plane`; require a planar deck + planar die (`roll {K}`) subsystem Wagic does not model.
Not part of any constructed/limited paper environment — promo/Planechase only.

- Stairs to Infinity (PRM)
- Celestine Reef (PRM)
- Horizon Boughs (PRM)
- Mirrored Depths (PRM)
- Tember City (PRM)
- Tazeem (PRM)

### E5 — "Most common color among all permanents"  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Requires a global tie-aware color-census the engine cannot compute as a filter.

- Tsabo's Assassin (INV) — destroy if shares most-common color
- Barrin's Unmaking (INV) — bounce if shares most-common color
- Heroic Defiance (PLS) — +3/+3 unless shares most-common color
- (see also Mana Maze under E6 for the "color of last spell" cousin)

### E6 — Cast-restriction / spell-color state locks  · ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM  (5)

Continuous restrictions keyed on other spells' colors or costs — no rules hook.

- Mana Maze (INV) — can't cast spell sharing color with last spell · ENGINE-BLOCKED
- Ice Cave (APC) — any player may pay a spell's full cost to counter · MEDIUM
- Gaddock Teeg (PRM) — noncreature CMC≥4 / with {X} can't be cast · ENGINE-BLOCKED
- Myr Superion (PRM) — "spend only mana produced by creatures" cast restriction · ENGINE-BLOCKED
- Emblazoned Golem (APC) — "spend only colored mana on X, one of each color" · ENGINE-BLOCKED

### E7 — Copy-spell-on-the-stack chains  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

"Copy this spell, choose new targets" and mass-copy — Wagic's stack-copy support is limited to a
few hard-coded cases (Willbender morph is the exception, itself unsupported here).

- Chain of Vapor (ONS, PRM)
- Chain of Plasma (ONS)
- Chain of Silence (ONS)
- Radiate (TOR) — copy for each other legal target
- Djinn Illuminatus (PRM) — grants replicate to all your instants/sorceries

### E8 — Counter/redirect abilities on the stack  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (6)

Countering *abilities* (not spells) and retargeting on the stack.

- Bind (INV) — counter target activated ability
- Stifle (PRM, SCG) — counter target activated *or triggered* ability
- Teferi's Response (INV) — counter opponent ability targeting your land
- Azorius Guildmage (PRM) — mode: counter target activated ability
- Deflection (7ED, 8ED) — change target of target spell
- Meddle (ONS) — conditional retarget of a creature-targeting spell

### E9 — "Change a target" / retarget effects  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (7)

Retargeting the top of the stack. (Willbender's `autofaceup=` line shows an attempted DSL form,
but the card is still catalogued unsupported.)

- Willbender (LGN, PRM)
- Quicksilver Dragon (ONS) — redirect single-target spell off itself
- Spellskite (PRM) — {PU}: change a target to Spellskite
- Mizzium Meddler (PRM) — ETB retarget
- Divert (ODY) — retarget unless controller pays {2}
- Mind Extraction* — (not retarget; see E15) 
- Alhammarret / name-lock (see E14)

### E10 — Prevention / damage-redirection shields  · MEDIUM  (many, 20)

"The next time a source of your choice would deal damage… prevent/redirect." Wagic's `prevent:`
primitive is partial; these need per-source, deferred, choose-a-source shields.

- Reverse Damage (7ED), Awe Strike (MRD) — prevent-then-gain-life
- Samite Ministration (INV), Protective Sphere (INV), Rhystic Circle (PCY), Story Circle (8ED),
  Mourner's Shield (MRD) — choose-a-source colored prevention
- Sphere of Law/Grace/Reason/Truth/Duty (ODY, 5 cards), Sphere of Purity (MRD),
  Divine Presence (INV) — flat colored/typed damage reduction/cap
- Kjeldoran Royal Guard (7ED), Mirror Strike (PCY), Beacon of Destiny (LGN),
  Zealous Inquisitor (SCG), Karona's Zealot (SCG) — combat-damage redirection to a permanent
- Aegis of Honor (ODY), Harsh Judgment (INV) — redirect instant/sorcery damage to its controller
- Pilgrim of Virtue / Pilgrim of Justice (ODY) — sac to prevent next colored damage
- Chain of Silence (ONS — also E7), Samite/Shieldmage overlap noted

### E11 — Replacement effects on damage / draw / life  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (9)

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of these names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — so this audit measures the *documented*
  unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. Cards these sets contain that Wagic
  *does* implement (the majority of each set) never appear in `missing_cards_by_sets` and are out
  of scope here.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards legitimately sit on an
  ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM boundary (noted inline). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text
  uses only mechanics proven elsewhere in the catalogue.
- The Odyssey-block keywords the task flagged (Flashback, Threshold, Madness) are indeed largely
  supported: only a handful of *flashback-rider* cards (Flaring Pain, Flash of Insight, Alter
  Reality, Catalyst Stone) surface here, and they are blocked by their *other* clause, not by
  flashback itself. No ANTE / CUMULATIVE-UPKEEP cards appear in these lists; the one BANDING card
  (Nalathni Dragon) and one SPLICE card (Glacial Ray) are noted as ENGINE-BLOCKED sub-mechanics.
