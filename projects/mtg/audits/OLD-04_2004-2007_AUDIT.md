As in the 2000–2003 era, essentially **the entire population is "known-broken, documented"**:
every name resolves to an `unsupported.txt` entry with full oracle text, grade *unsupported*
(does not load, not AI-deck safe). **None are stale** — nothing in these lists silently already
works. The one true absence is a quirk of a quote-marked Unhinged card name and is out of scope
by nature anyway.

The dominant story of this era is **two large OUT-OF-SCOPE / ENGINE-BLOCKED blocs**:

1. **Unhinged (UNH) — 123 silver-border joke cards.** Non-tournament-legal by design; most rely
   on real-world actions (speaking, physical objects, opening booster packs, card-name puns, art
   references). All OUT-OF-SCOPE per the README's "not a distinct playable paper card" bucket.
2. **Splice onto Arcane (Kamigawa block: CHK/BOK/SOK, ~34 cards).** Splice is an unsupported
   sub-mechanic — the card is a normal spell, but the *splice half* (reveal from hand, pay splice
   cost, graft its effects onto an Arcane spell) has no Wagic hook. These are grouped once below.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

### E1 — Unhinged silver-border joke cards · OUT-OF-SCOPE (123)

The entire UNH file plus its PAL04 reprints. Silver-border, never legal in constructed/limited
paper; the mechanics are real-world/humor gimmicks (speak a word, physically stack cards, guess,
open a real booster, "longest name ever," fractions, ASCII art). Not distinct playable digital
cards. A representative sample of the *kinds* of un-mechanic:

- **Speak / real-world-action triggers:** Goblin Mime (PAL04, UNH — "when you speak"), Loose Lips,
  Frankie Peanuts, Ladies' Knight, Bosom Buddy, Now I Know My ABC's, B-I-N-G-O, Cheatyface,
  Enter the Dungeon (play a subgame under the table).
- **Open a real booster / metagame:** Booster Tutor (PAL04, UNH), Rare-B-Gone, Aesthetic
  Consultation, R&D's Secret Lair, Look at Me I'm R&D, Fascist Art Director, Richard Garfield Ph.D.
- **Puns / typography / card-name gags:** Our Market Research…Longest Card Name Ever Elemental,
  Who/What/When/Where/Why, `_____`, Ambiguity, Name Dropping, Wordmail, Atinlay Igpay, Number
  Crunch, Fraction Jackson, Symbol Status, Pointy Finger of Doom.
- **Physical dexterity / props:** Water Gun Balloon Game, Rod of Spanking, Toy Boat, Letter Bomb,
  Assquatch, Head to Head, Eye to Eye, Mouth to Mouth, Side to Side, Face to Face, Touch and Go.
- **Half-baked "real" cards that still need un-rules** (Old Fogey with vestigial vintage keywords,
  Greater Morphling, Yet Another Aether Vortex, Zzzyxas's Abyss, etc.).

All 123 are counted once here and not re-listed under any other reason. (Full list in
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/UNH.txt` + the four UNH-reprint rows of `PAL04.txt`.)

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of these names resolve to `unsupported.txt` (grade *unsupported* → does not load, not
  AI-deck safe), **0 stale**. This audit measures the *documented* unsupported set for the era, not
  raw Scryfall completeness — the majority of each set that Wagic *does* implement never appears in
  `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope here.
- **Unhinged (UNH):** treated wholesale as OUT-OF-SCOPE. Its cards are not tournament-legal paper
  cards in the sense the README's "distinct playable paper card" test requires; the ~123 are
  counted once (E1) and not double-tiered. A handful (Greater Morphling, Old Fogey, Zzzyxas's
  Abyss) are *nearly* functional but still carry silver-border un-rules — not worth peeling out.
- **Splice onto Arcane** (Kamigawa block) is the single biggest *real-card* blocker of the era at
  ~34 distinct names; it is one shared ENGINE-BLOCKED sub-mechanic. Implementing splice once would
  unlock the whole group (the non-splice halves are otherwise simple spells).
- **Cumulative upkeep** (Coldsnap, 7) and **split second** (Time Spiral, 1 pure — Trickbind) are
  the other new ENGINE-BLOCKED keywords. Base **suspend** and base **morph** *are* supported; only
  the *payoff/manipulation riders* (time-counter add/remove, turn-up triggers) surface here.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics proven
  elsewhere in the catalogue (Three Dreams, Auratouched Mage — Aura tutors).
- The one truly-absent name (`"Ach! Hans, Run!"`, UNH) is a lookup artifact of embedded quote marks
  in a joke card name; it is OUT-OF-SCOPE regardless.
