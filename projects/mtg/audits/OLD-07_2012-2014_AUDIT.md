# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2012–2014 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**PC2 · M13 · IVG · RTR · CM1 · PI13 · GTC · DGM · MMA · M14 · THS · C13 · BNG · JOU · CNS · VMA**
(Planechase 2012, Magic 2013, Duels of the Planeswalkers 2012 promos, Return to Ravnica,
Commander's Arsenal, Duels of the Planeswalkers 2013 promos, Gatecrash, Dragon's Maze,
Modern Masters, Magic 2014, Theros, Commander 2013, Born of the Gods, Journey into Nyx,
Conspiracy, Vintage Masters).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the peer era docs. Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the
underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no supported example exists.

**110 of 112** cards in these missing-lists are **catalogued-unsupported** entries: upstream

→ does not load, not AI-deck safe) but never wrote a working primitive. **2 are STALE** — they now
resolve to a `borderline` primitive and quietly work, so they should be dropped from the
missing-list (see below). None are true dangling references outside the catalogue. As with the
2007–2009 era, the whole population is "known-broken, documented" — plus two that have since been
fixed and never removed from the list.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Will of the council (voting)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (many, 10)

No voting subsystem exists — every probed "will of the council" card is unsupported. Each needs a
simultaneous per-player vote among named choices, with tie-handling. This is the single biggest
engine gap the Conspiracy / Vintage Masters lists surface.

- Council's Judgment (CNS, VMA) — vote a nonland permanent you don't control, exile the winner(s)
- Coercive Portal (CNS, VMA) — upkeep vote carnage/homage → wrath-or-draw
- Magister of Worth (CNS, VMA) — ETB vote grace/condemnation → mass-reanimate or wrath
- Tyrant's Choice (CNS, VMA) — vote death/torture → sac a creature or lose 4
- Plea for Power (CNS, VMA) — vote time/knowledge → extra turn or draw 3
- Custodi Squire (CNS) — ETB vote a card in your GY, return the winner(s)
- Council Guardian (CNS) — vote a color, gains protection from the winner(s)
- Split Decision (CNS) — vote denial/duplication → counter or copy the target spell
- Bite of the Black Rose (CNS) — vote sickness/psychosis → -2/-2 or discard 2
- Grudge Keeper (CNS) — voting payoff: each opposing voter loses 2 life (depends on the vote engine)

### E2 — Draft-matters / hidden-agenda (Conspiracy)  · OUT-OF-SCOPE  (many, 13)

These cards interact with the **draft itself** (reveal/note/pass cards during drafting) or start
face-up in the command zone as a Conspiracy. Wagic has no draft-time card-interaction layer, and
the draft-mode work in this fork does not model per-card draft triggers. Not distinct playable
constructed cards inside the duel engine — grouped OUT-OF-SCOPE.

- Paliano, the High City (CNS) — draft-time color-vote land
- Agent of Acquisitions (CNS) — "draft the whole pack" at draft time
- Aether Searcher (CNS) — note the next drafted card's name; cast it free on ETB
- Canal Dredger (CNS) — redirects last-card-of-pack passing during the draft
- Deal Broker (CNS) — post-draft card-pool swap
- Cogwork Tracker (CNS) — note who passed it; must attack that player
- Cogwork Spy (CNS) — peek at the next drafted card
- Cogwork Librarian (CNS) — draft an extra card, put itself back in the pack
- Cogwork Grinder (CNS) — remove cards from the draft face down; counters = removed
- Lurking Automaton (CNS) — counters = how many cards you'd drafted this round
- Lore Seeker (CNS) — add a booster pack to the draft
- Whispergear Sneak (CNS) — look at unopened packs during the draft
- Power Play (CNS) — Conspiracy: you are the starting player (command-zone start)

### E3 — Copy an activated/triggered ability on the stack  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Copying a *spell* is supported (Twincast), but there is no primitive to copy an **ability** already
on the stack (Strionic Resonator, Illusionist's Bracers, Rings of Brighthearth all unsupported).

- Strionic Resonator (CM1) — {2},{T}: copy a triggered ability you control
- Illusionist's Bracers (GTC) — copy the activated ability of equipped creature

### E4 — Exchange / swap control of two permanents (or a spell)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Two-way control exchange — "gain control of A and give away B" — has no working primitive (every
example unsupported). Includes the "swap this enchantment for what it targets" pattern.

- Conjured Currency (RTR) — upkeep: exchange control of itself and a target permanent
- Perplexing Chimera (BNG) — exchange control of itself and an opponent's spell on the stack
- Daring Thief (JOU) — inspired: exchange a permanent you control for a same-type one an opponent controls
- Order of Succession (C13) — table-wide directional creature-control pass

### E5 — Tempting offer  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / STALE  (5; 2 already done)

"Tempting offer" partially works: **Tempt with Glory** and **Tempt with Vengeance** now resolve to
borderline primitives (STALE — remove from list). The remaining three are still unsupported and
need the "each opponent may copy the effect; you get it again per opponent who does" loop.

- Tempt with Discovery (C13) — land-tutor tempting offer · MEDIUM
- Tempt with Reflections (C13) — token-copy tempting offer · MEDIUM
- Tempt with Immortality (C13) — reanimate tempting offer · MEDIUM
- Tempt with Glory (C13) — +1/+1 counters — **STALE (borderline, works)**
- Tempt with Vengeance (C13) — Elemental tokens — **STALE (borderline, works)**

### E6 — Splice onto Arcane  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

No splice primitive exists (all Kamigawa splice cards unsupported; Kodama's Reach only "works"
because it's a plain tutor with no splice behaviour). Reprinted en masse in Modern Masters.

- Horobi's Whisper (MMA) — splice onto Arcane, exile 4 from GY
- Desperate Ritual (MMA) — splice onto Arcane {1}{R}, ritual
- Dampen Thought (MMA) — splice onto Arcane {1}{U}, mill 4
- Glacial Ray (MMA) — splice onto Arcane {1}{R}, 2 damage
- Torrent of Stone (MMA) — splice onto Arcane, sac two Mountains, 4 damage

### E7 — Opponent-partitions two-pile choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

The "an opponent separates cards into two piles; you pick one" family (or its "you split, opponent
chooses" mirror). No partition UI. (The *self-split-you-choose* direction — Fact or Fiction — IS
supported, which is why these specific cards fall out as unsupported.)

- Sphinx of Uthuun (IVG) — ETB: opponent splits your top 5 into two piles
- Steam Augury (CM1) — you reveal 5 and split; opponent chooses the pile you keep
- Gifts Ungiven (MMA) — tutor 4 different names; opponent chooses two to bin

### E8 — Cipher spells blocked by their body  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (many, 12)

Cipher itself is supported (Trepanation Blade, Nightveil Specter). Every card here is blocked by
the *spell it ciphers* — the encoded effect uses a clause the cipher primitive doesn't wire up
(token-copy, text-change, exile-and-return, sacrifice-a-permanent, wheel, etc.). RTR/GTC/DGM carry
all of these.

- Last Thoughts (GTC) — draw + cipher
- Call of the Nightwing (GTC) — 1/1 flyer token + cipher
- Stolen Identity (GTC) — token copy of artifact/creature + cipher (also token-copy, E10)
- Hands of Binding (GTC) — tap + don't-untap + cipher
- Paranoid Delusions (GTC) — mill 3 + cipher
- Shadow Slice (GTC) — opponent loses 3 + cipher
- Undercity Plague (GTC) — lose life / discard / sacrifice + cipher
- Voidwalk (GTC) — exile a creature, return at end step + cipher
- Midnight Recovery (GTC) — GY creature to hand + cipher
- Mental Vapors (GTC) — target discards + cipher
- Whispering Madness (GTC) — wheel + cipher
- Hidden Strings (DGM) — tap/untap two permanents + cipher

### E9 — Fight with a rider / directed fight  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

Plain fight is supported (Prey Upon, Pit Fight). These bolt extra machinery onto the fight —
opponent-chosen participant, upkeep-triggered dual reveal-and-fight, or a modal fight.

- Rivals' Duel (PC2) — two creatures that share no types fight
- Magus of the Arena (C13) — tap your creature + an opponent's *chosen* creature, they fight
- Guild Feud (RTR) — upkeep: both players cheat a creature into play, then those fight
- Marath, Will of the Wild (C13) — modal counter-sink; one mode deals X damage (also needs ETB-counters = mana spent)

### E10 — Copy-a-creature variants blocked by their source clause  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

"Enters as a copy" is supported (Clone, Phantasmal Image). These pull the copy target from a
graveyard, a milled zone, or the top of a graveyard continuously.

- The Mimeoplasm (CM1) — enters as a copy of one exiled GY creature + counters from the other · MEDIUM
- Stolen Identity (GTC) — token that's a copy of a target (also cipher, E8) · MEDIUM
- Volrath's Shapeshifter (VMA) — continuously has the full text of the top GY creature card · ENGINE-BLOCKED (live top-of-GY text copy)
- Lazav, Dimir Mastermind (GTC) — becomes a copy of any creature milled to an opponent's GY · ENGINE-BLOCKED (event-driven live copy)

### E11 — Counter an ability / counter-by-name  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Counter-target-*spell* is supported; counter-an-*ability* has only one borderline exemplar
(Voidslime), and counter-by-stored-name needs the exile-and-remember loop.

- Voidmage Husher (PI13) — ETB counter target activated ability + self-bounce
- Mindreaver (BNG) — heroic exile-3; sac to counter a spell sharing a name with those exiled

### E12 — Prevention / damage-redirection shields  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Wagic's `prevent:` primitive is partial; these need choose-a-source, devotion-scaled, or
redirect-to-another-permanent shields (all probed examples unsupported).

- Acolyte's Reward (BNG) — prevent X (= devotion to white), redirect that much as damage
- Kor Chant (CNS) — redirect all damage from a chosen source to another creature
- Zhalfirin Crusader (VMA) — flanking (supported) + redirect next 1 damage to another target

### E13 — Text-changing effects  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"Replace all instances of one color/land-type word." No text-substitution layer.

- Trait Doctoring (DGM) — change a color/land word on a permanent + cipher

### E14 — Control a player's turn  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"You control target player during their next turn" — no take-over-opponent framework (Mindslaver
family, all unsupported).

- Worst Fears (JOU) — control target player's next turn, then exile

### E15 — Cast-from-exile "free spells" engines  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Casting exiled cards free is supported in narrow forms; these need X-scaled exile-then-cast loops
or "cast the same-type card you just exiled" replacement.

- Epic Experiment (RTR) — exile top X, cast all instants/sorceries with CMC ≤ X free · MEDIUM
- Jeleva, Nephalia's Scourge (C13) — ETB exile top X (= mana spent) from each library; cast on attack · MEDIUM
- Search the City (RTR) — exile top 5; recur on name-match; take an extra turn when empty · MEDIUM
- Possibility Storm (DGM) — replace every hand-cast: exile it, dig for a shared-type card, cast that · ENGINE-BLOCKED (cast-replacement)

### E16 — Blocker-count / attack-restriction statics  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Static overrides of how many creatures can attack/block, or forced who-can-be-blocked-by. No
blocker-count override in the combat engine (Crawlspace, Mystic Barrier, Valor Made Real,
Tromokratis all unsupported).

- Crawlspace (C13) — no more than two creatures can attack you each combat
- Mystic Barrier (C13) — each player may attack only one direction (table geometry)
- Valor Made Real (CNS) — target creature can block any number of creatures
- Tromokratis (BNG) — can't be blocked unless *all* the defender's creatures block it

### E17 — Named-card cast lock  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

"Choose a name; opponents can't cast cards with that name" (and its exile-to-lock variant). No
name-based player restriction (Council of the Absolute, Godsend, Runed Halo all unsupported).

- Council of the Absolute (DGM) — name a noncreature/nonland; opponents can't cast it, you pay {2} less
- Godsend (JOU) — exile blockers/blocked creatures; opponents can't cast their names

### E18 — Reveal-and-choose-at-random search  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Search + reveal is supported, but "choose one at random" among the revealed / total-CMC-of-revealed
needs a randomizer over a chosen set.

- Signal the Clans (GTC) — tutor 3 different-named creatures; keep one at random
- Naya Soulbeast (C13) — each player reveals top card; counters = total CMC revealed

### E19 — Aura mass-exile-and-reattach  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

"Exile the creature and all its Auras, return them all attached." Faith's Reward (mass return) is
supported, but the note-the-Auras-and-reattach bookkeeping is not.

- Flickerform (C13) — blink enchanted creature + all its Auras, reattach at end step

### E20 — Suspend self-recursion / unusual time-counter play  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Base suspend is supported (Ancestral Vision, Rift Bolt). These re-suspend *themselves* from the
graveyard or let opponents manipulate the suspend counter.

- Epochrasite (MMA) — dies → exile with 3 time counters, gains suspend (self-recursion)
- Pardic Dragon (MMA) — suspend + opponents may *add* time counters while suspended

### E21 — Dig / reorder library loops  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Iterative "look, pay, reorder, repeat" library manipulation. No pay-to-loop dig primitive
(Scroll Rack, Lim-Dul's Vault unsupported).

- Scroll Rack (CM1) — swap any number of hand cards for the top of library, reorder exiled ones
- Lim-Dul's Vault (C13) — pay-1-life reorder loop over the top 5

### E22 — Replicate granted to every spell  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Replicate itself has borderline exemplars (Pyromatics), but granting it to *every* instant/sorcery
you cast, with the cost = that spell's mana cost, is a global cost/copy layer the primitive can't
author.

- Djinn Illuminatus (M13) — each instant/sorcery you cast has replicate = its mana cost

### E23 — Counter-a-spell "steal it" replacement  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Counter-target-spell is supported; the "put the countered card onto the battlefield under your
control instead" replacement is not.

- Desertion (CM1) — counter a spell; steal an artifact/creature spell into play

### E24 — Miscellaneous single-card oddities  · mixed

- Three Dreams (PC2) — tutor three different Aura cards to hand · **BACKLOG-EASY** (multi-tutor with different-names constraint)
- Auratouched Mage (PC2) — ETB tutor an Aura that could enchant it, attach or reveal · MEDIUM (Aura auto-attach on ETB)
- Phylactery Lich (M13) — ETB phylactery counter on an artifact; indestructible; sac when none remain · MEDIUM
- Whims of the Fates (BNG) — each player thirds their board, sacrifices one third at random · ENGINE-BLOCKED (self-partition + random pile, per-player)
- Vortex Elemental (BNG) — activated: shuffle itself + combatants into libraries · MEDIUM
- Soul Ransom (GTC) — steal-a-creature aura; "only an opponent may activate" the sac-to-draw ability · MEDIUM
- Boros Battleshaper (DGM) — combat: force-attack/block one + can't-attack/block one · MEDIUM
- Varolz, the Scar-Striped (DGM) — grants scavenge to your GY creatures + regen (scavenge is supported; the *grant* layer is the gap) · MEDIUM
- Vizkopa Confessor (GTC) — extort (supported) + pay-life ETB hand-exile · MEDIUM
- Tablet of the Guilds (RTR) — choose-two-colors, lifegain per chosen color of spells cast · MEDIUM
- Illusionist's Gambit (C13) — combat fog + forced second combat with redirected attackers · ENGINE-BLOCKED (extra-combat re-declaration)
- Serene Master (C13) — on block, exchange power with the blocked creature · MEDIUM
- Capricious Efreet (C13) — upkeep: choose 3 permanents, destroy one at random · ENGINE-BLOCKED (random destroy among chosen)
- From the Ashes (C13) — destroy nonbasic lands, each controller may fetch basics · MEDIUM

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of the 110 excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt`, and the 2 stale names to
  `borderline.txt` — so this audit measures the *documented* unsupported set for the era, not raw
  Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set (everything Wagic *does* implement) never appears in
  `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope here.
- The era's two block mechanics are **overwhelmingly supported** — cipher, extort, evolve, populate,
  detain, unleash, scavenge (RtR block) and devotion, bestow, heroic, monstrosity, tribute, inspired
  (Theros block) all have supported exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is why Theros contributes
  only **one** missing card (a copy-ability slip-through) and why so many RtR-block cards land in
  BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by a rider") rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED. The single largest BACKLOG group
  is **GTC/DGM cipher cards whose only real gap is the enciphered spell's body**.
- The single largest ENGINE-BLOCKED group is **Conspiracy "will of the council" voting** — there is
  no voting UI or multi-player simultaneous-choice framework, so every voting card is unsupported.
  The **draft-matters / hidden-agenda** cards are filed OUT-OF-SCOPE rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED
  because they act on the draft, not the duel, and are not distinct playable constructed cards in the
  engine's sense.
- **PC2 (Planechase 2012) contributes zero `type=Plane` cards** to its missing-list — the three PC2
  entries are ordinary cards (an Aura tutor, a fight sorcery, an Aura-fetch creature). So, as in the
  2007–2009 era, there is **no OUT-OF-SCOPE planar bucket** here; the planar-subsystem gap is real
  but simply does not surface in `missing_cards_by_sets/PC2.txt`.
- **CM1 (Commander's Arsenal) and MMA (Modern Masters)** are reprint products; their missing cards
  are genuine mechanic gaps (copy-ability, two-pile, dig-loop; splice-onto-Arcane) reprinted from
  earlier sets, not stale duplicates of implemented names. Verified: none of them resolve to a
  supported/borderline primitive.
- Two cards are **STALE** and should be dropped from `C13.txt`: **Tempt with Glory** and **Tempt
  with Vengeance** now resolve to borderline primitives and load/play. The other three Tempt cards
  remain unsupported, so tempting-offer is only partially landed.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline — e.g. Volrath's Shapeshifter, Possibility Storm). No card was moved to
  EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics proven elsewhere in the catalogue (only Three Dreams
  qualified).
