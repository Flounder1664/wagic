# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2016–2017 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**EMN · CN2 · MPS · KLD · C16 · PZ2 · PCA · AER · MM3 · DDS · AKH · MP2 · CMA · E01 · HOU · C17**
(Eldritch Moon, Conspiracy: Take the Crown, Kaladesh Inventions, Kaladesh, Commander 2016,
Planechase Anthology 2, Planechase Anthology [PCA reprint pool], Aether Revolt, Modern Masters
2017, Duel Decks: Mind vs. Might, Amonkhet, Amonkhet Invocations, Commander Anthology, Archenemy:
Nicol Bolas, Hour of Devastation, Commander 2017).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the SOS backlog and the 2007–2009 era doc. Before any card was
tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no
supported example exists.

excluded population is "known-broken, documented."

### A note on PZ2 / PCA and "type=Plane"

PZ2 (Planechase Anthology 2) and PCA (Planechase Anthology) are planar products, and the brief
flagged `type=Plane` cards as OUT-OF-SCOPE. **But neither missing-list contains a single `type=Plane`
card** — every entry is an ordinary spell/creature (the Plane subsystem gap simply does not surface
in `missing_cards_by_sets`). This is the same pattern the 2007–2009 doc found for HOP (Planechase):
the planar gap is real but invisible here. So there is **no OUT-OF-SCOPE Plane bucket** in this era.
The PZ2/PCA/C16/CMA/C17 entries are the multiplayer Commander staples (voting, join-forces,
tempting-offer, "gift a permanent to an opponent") that Wagic's 1v1 engine has no framework for.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Each card is
filed once, at its **hardest** blocker. Tier tags: **ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) ·
**BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Voting / council's dilemma / will of the council  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (11)

No voting primitive exists — every council/vote/dilemma card in the index is unsupported. Requires a
per-player simultaneous ballot UI and a tally-driven effect the engine cannot express.

- Messenger Jays (CN2) — council's dilemma: feather/quill → counters or loot
- Capital Punishment (CN2) — council's dilemma: death/taxes → sac or discard
- Lieutenants of the Guard (CN2) — council's dilemma: strength/numbers → counter or token
- Selvala's Stampede (CN2, PZ2) — council's dilemma: wild/free → reveal-into-play or free permanent
- Orchard Elemental (CN2) — council's dilemma: sprout/harvest → counters or life
- Expropriate (CN2, PZ2) — council's dilemma: time/money → extra turns or mass control-steal
- Custodi Squire (PZ2) — will of the council: vote a GY card, return most-voted (also GY-return)
- Ballot Broker (CN2) — "while voting, you may vote an additional time" (pure voting-support static)
- (Custodi Peacekeeper is E2 — its "note during draft" is the harder blocker)

The three above with a **draft-matters** clause on top are filed in E2. The pure-voting set is:

- Messenger Jays (CN2), Capital Punishment (CN2), Lieutenants of the Guard (CN2),
  Selvala's Stampede (CN2, PZ2), Orchard Elemental (CN2), Expropriate (CN2, PZ2),
  Custodi Squire (PZ2), and the Cruel Entertainment / Cruel-style "control a player's turn" pieces
  are split out to E3.

*(Distinct pure-voting cards: Messenger Jays, Capital Punishment, Lieutenants of the Guard,
Selvala's Stampede, Orchard Elemental, Expropriate, Custodi Squire, Ballot Broker = 8. The remaining
"dilemma" cards that also touch draft/turn-control are counted in E2/E3 to avoid double-listing.)*

### E2 — Conspiracy "draft matters" (draft face-up / note / hidden agenda)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (13)

These read the **draft** itself — "draft this face up," "as you draft a card, note its name/type,"
"remove a card from the draft," "reveal as you draft and guess the next pick." Wagic's booster draft
has no hidden-agenda / face-up / note layer, and there is no supported exemplar. Even if the draft
support existed, the in-game payoff keys on drafted-card state the engine never stores.

- Archdemon of Paliano (CN2) — draft face up, random-draft lock for 3 picks
- Spire Phantasm (CN2) — reveal as drafted, guess the next drafter's pick
- Paliano Vanguard (CN2) — note creature types drafted, buff those types
- Noble Banneret (CN2) — note names drafted, lord + lifelink to matching creatures
- Custodi Peacekeeper (CN2) — note draft-round position; tap by highest noted number
- Illusionary Informant (CN2) — look at another player's next drafted card
- Arcane Savant (CN2) — exile an instant/sorcery you drafted, ETB free-cast a copy
- Leovold's Operative (CN2) — draft an extra card, skip the next pack
- Smuggler Captain (CN2) — note a drafted name, ETB tutor it
- Regicide (CN2) — three drafters each choose a color; destroy a creature of those colors
- Garbage Fire (CN2) — note draft-round position; damage = highest noted number
- Caller of the Untamed (CN2) — exile a drafted creature, activated token-copy of it
- Animus of Predation (CN2) — remove drafted creatures, inherit their keywords
- Volatile Chimera (CN2) — exile 3+ drafted creatures, become a random one
- Pyretic Hunter (CN2) — note draft-round position; ETB with that many counters

*(15 CN2 draft-matters cards. This is the single largest bucket of the era — Conspiracy: Take the
Crown is fundamentally a draft-format-defined set that Wagic's engine cannot host.)*

### E3 — Control an opponent's turn (Mindslaver-style)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

"You control target player during that player's next turn." No supported example (Mindslaver, Worst
Fears both unsupported). Requires driving another player's whole turn — no framework.

- Cruel Entertainment (C16) — two players swap control of each other's next turn
- Emrakul, the Promised End (EMN) — on cast, control target opponent during their next turn (plus a
  CMC-graveyard cost reduction and pro-instants — the turn-control is the hard blocker)

### E4 — Join forces  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

"Starting with you, each player may pay any amount of mana." A cooperative simultaneous-payment
prompt across all players; no supported exemplar (Alliance of Arms, Shared Trauma unsupported).

- Minds Aglow (C16) — join forces → each player draws X
- Collective Voyage (C16) — join forces → each player ramps X basics
- Mana-Charged Dragon (CMA) — join forces on attack/block → +X/+0

### E5 — Tempting offer  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Tempting offer IS partially supported (Tempt with Vengeance, Tempt with Glory both borderline), so
this is a rider/transcription gap, not an engine wall. (Tempt with Glory itself is **STALE** — already
borderline.)

- Tempt with Discovery (C16) — tempting offer: land tutor, doubled per opponent who accepts

### E6 — "Has all activated abilities of cards in exile / with cage counters"  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

A creature that grants itself the activated abilities of an arbitrary pool of exiled cards. No
primitive can copy a variable set of abilities off other cards' text (Mairsil, Dark Impostor,
Havengul Lich, Skill Borrower, Necrotic Ooze all unsupported).

- Mairsil, the Pretender (C17) — cage-counter exile pool, use each ability once/turn
- Dark Impostor (C17) — exile creatures, gain all their activated abilities
- Havengul Lich (C17) — cast a GY creature, gain its activated abilities that turn
- Torchling (E01) — five different {R}/{1} self-modification abilities (transcription-heavy multi-mode; filed here as a same-family "wall of activated abilities" card — see also E19)

*(Torchling is really a multi-mode utility creature; it is counted once in E19 to keep E6 to the
true "copy-abilities-from-exile" trio: Mairsil, Dark Impostor, Havengul Lich = 3.)*

### E7 — Cipher  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"Exile this spell card encoded on a creature; recast on combat damage." No cipher primitive (Stolen
Identity, Hidden Strings, Shadow Slice all unsupported).

- Whispering Madness (C16) — wheel effect + cipher

### E8 — Copy-a-spell "for each legal target" (Radiate-style)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Single-target copy is supported (Twincast/Fork). Copying a spell once per other legal target is not
(Radiate unsupported).

- Mirrorwing Dragon (EMN) — when a spell targets only it, copy for each other creature you control

### E9 — Mass live-copy (every other creature becomes a copy)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Base ETB-clone is supported (Clone/Body Double), but making *every other* creature simultaneously
become a copy of one target is a mass live-copy the primitives never captured (Mirrorweave
unsupported in prior eras too).

- Mirrorweave (C16) — each other creature becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature EOT

### E10 — Copy-a-creature riders (attack-copy, exile-and-return)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

ETB-clone is supported; this one carries an attack trigger + exile-a-creature + return-at-end-step
recursion.

- Identity Thief (EMN) — on attack, exile another creature and become a copy of it until EOT

### E11 — Opponent partitions objects into piles / opponent binary choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

The "two-pile" family — an opponent divides revealed cards and you (or they) pick a pile, or an
opponent makes a binary you-vs-them choice. Your-own-split (Fact or Fiction) is borderline, but the
opponent-driven partition/choice variants are unsupported (Gifts Ungiven, Brilliant Ultimatum,
Covenant of Minds). No partition UI.

- Fortune's Favor (EMN) — opponent splits your top 4 into face-up/face-down piles; you pick
- Dubious Challenge (KLD) — opponent picks one of two exiled creatures to keep, you get the rest
- Manifold Insights (C16) — each opponent chooses a different nonland card from your top 10
- Covenant of Minds (CN2) — opponent chooses: hand you 3, or you mill-3-and-draw-5
- Gifts Ungiven (DDS) — tutor 4 different names; opponent chooses which 2 go to GY

### E12 — Whims/piles: split-your-own-permanents-and-sacrifice  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Random pile selection over your own board — needs a partition + random-pick, but self-driven, so
MEDIUM rather than engine-blocked.

- Whims of the Fates (C16) — each player splits their permanents into 3 piles, sac a random one

### E13 — Triggered-ability doubling (Panharmonicon family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

A global "your ETB/triggered abilities trigger an additional time" multiplier. No supported example
(Panharmonicon, Strionic Resonator, Yarok all unsupported).

- Panharmonicon (KLD) — artifact/creature ETB triggers fire one extra time

### E14 — Coin flip / escalating flip loop  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No coin-flip primitive (Krark's Thumb, Mana Clash, Fiery Gambit unsupported).

- Game of Chaos (PZ2) — repeated coin flip, doubling life stakes each flip

### E15 — "End the turn" / turn-truncation  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Exile everything from the stack and end the turn — no turn-truncation primitive (Time Stop, Sundial,
Discontinuity all unsupported).

- Glorious End (AKH) — end the turn now, then lose the game next end step

### E16 — Counter by unusual condition  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Plain "counter target spell" is supported. Each of these keys on a hidden-zone comparison or a
redirect, and Counterbalance's cost-match has no supported analog at all.

- Counterbalance (MP2) — reveal top card, counter if CMCs match · leans ENGINE-BLOCKED (cost-match on reveal)
- Desertion (CN2) — counter, and steal a countered artifact/creature to the battlefield · MEDIUM
- Divert (MP2) — change a single-target spell's target unless controller pays {2} · MEDIUM

### E17 — Counter an activated/triggered ability  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Partly supported (Disallow/Voidslime borderline); the plain-vanilla Stifle is unsupported, so this
is a transcription/edge gap rather than an engine wall.

- Stifle (MP2) — counter target activated or triggered ability

### E18 — Gain-control auras with a give-away / opponent-activates rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Straight control-steal auras are supported (Control Magic). These invert or gift the control, or let
the *opponent* activate an ability — the duration/ownership rider is what blocks them.

- Soul Ransom (MM3, E01) — control enchanted creature; **only an opponent** may pay to break it
- Zedruu the Greathearted (C16) — donate your permanents to opponents for card/life engine
- Assault Suit (C16, CMA) — equip that lets each opponent borrow the creature each upkeep

### E19 — Multi-mode / multi-clause utility permanents  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (many)

Marquee mechanic (if any) is supported; the card is blocked by a dense multi-clause body — cost
reduction stacked with a second trigger, conditional replacement effects, "note a value then use it,"
manifest riders, etc. All authorable in the DSL with care; none hit an engine wall.

- Foul Emissary (EMN) — ETB dig-for-creature + emerge-sacrifice token trigger
- Nahiri's Wrath (EMN) — discard-X additional cost, X-target damage = discarded CMCs
- Soul Separator (EMN) — split a GY creature into a Spirit copy + a Zombie body
- Chain of Vapor (C16) — bounce + optional sac-land to copy-and-retarget
- Ghastly Conscription (C16) — exile a GY, shuffle, manifest the pile (manifest supported; the
  face-down-pile-from-GY sequencing is the rider)
- Divergent Transformations (C16) — undaunted + exile two, each reveals-into-play a creature
- Oath of Druids (C16) — each upkeep, behind-on-creatures reveal-into-play
- Baral, Chief of Compliance (AER) — cost reduction (supported) + counter-triggered loot
- Inspiring Statuary (AER) — grants **improvise** to your nonartifact spells
- Crackdown Construct (AER) — pump on activating an artifact/creature's nonmana ability
- Pia's Revolution (AER) — artifact-death → return-to-hand unless opponent takes 3
- Dark Intimations (AER) — multi-clause value spell + Bolas-planeswalker tie-in
- Midnight Oil (KLD) — hour-counter draw engine + hand-size cap + discard-life clause
- Lost Legacy (KLD) — name a card, exile from GY+hand+library, draw per hand-exile
- Spark of Creativity (KLD) — impulse-damage-or-play top card
- Captured by the Consulate (KLD) — aura: can't-attack + redirect single-target spells to it
- Flickerform (CMA) — aura: exile enchanted creature + all its Auras, return with the Auras reattached
- Selfless Squire (C16, PZ2) — prevent all damage to you this turn, convert prevented amount to +1/+1 counters
- Frontier Siege (C17) — mode-choice ramp OR flyer-fight-on-ETB
- Monastery Siege (C17) — mode-choice loot OR spell-tax
- Divine Reckoning (C17) — each player keeps one creature; flashback
- Portal Mage (C17) — reselect an attacker's defending player/planeswalker
- Alms Collector (PZ2, C17) — opponent draw-two → replacement: each draws one
- Torchling (E01) — five modal {R}/{1} self-modification abilities (untap, redirect, +1/-1, etc.)
- Havengul Lich → filed in E6 (ability-grant is the harder blocker)

## Confidence & caveats

- 103 of the 105 distinct names resolve to `unsupported.txt`; the other 2 (Oreskos Explorer, Tempt
  with Glory) resolve to `borderline.txt` and are **STALE** — already working, wrongly in the
  missing-list. As always, this audit measures the *documented* unsupported set for the era, not raw
  Scryfall completeness; the bulk of each set (everything Wagic *does* implement) never appears in
  `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope.
- The era's marquee mechanics are **overwhelmingly supported** — energy, fabricate, vehicles/crew,
  improvise, embalm, eternalize, exert, aftermath, afflict, manifest, and splice all have supported
  or borderline exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is exactly why the KLD/AER/AKH/HOU missing
  counts are tiny (7/5/2/1) and why the cards that *are* missing land in BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by
  a rider") rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED.
- The **engine-blocked mass is concentrated in the multiplayer-Commander / Conspiracy products** —
  CN2 alone contributes 22 of the ~40 engine-blocked cards (15 draft-matters + 7 voting). These are
  format-defining mechanics (hidden-agenda draft, council's dilemma, join forces, "control a
  player's turn," donate-a-permanent) that a 1v1 engine has no analog for. Genuinely engine-blocked
  patterns confirmed by absence of any supported example: **voting/council's-dilemma**,
  **conspiracy draft-matters**, **join forces**, **Mindslaver-style turn control**, **cipher**,
  **coin flip**, **"end the turn,"** **Panharmonicon trigger-doubling**, **Radiate-style
  copy-per-target**, **mass live-copy**, **opponent-partition piles**, **copy-abilities-from-exile**,
  and **secret hidden choice**.
- **PZ2 and PCA (Planechase Anthology 1 & 2) contribute zero `type=Plane` cards** to these
  missing-lists — same as HOP in the 2007–2009 doc. The planar subsystem gap is real but simply
  doesn't surface in `missing_cards_by_sets`, so there is **no OUT-OF-SCOPE Plane bucket** this era.
  The premium reprint products (MPS, MP2 = Masterpiece/Invocation foils; MM3, CMA, DDS = Modern
  Masters / Commander Anthology / duel decks) add no new mechanics; their handful of missing cards
  are ordinary reprints filed by their real blocker and deduped against the parent set.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline — e.g. Counterbalance's cost-match, Ghastly Conscription's face-down-pile
  manifest). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text reduced to a single proven one-liner —
  none did this era.
