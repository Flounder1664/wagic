## The shape of this era: one giant OUT-OF-SCOPE set + a thin reprint tail

This era is dominated, by raw volume, by a single set: **UST (Unstable)** — the silver-border
"joke" set — contributes **191 distinct cards**, and *every one of them is OUT-OF-SCOPE*. UST's
mechanics are structurally outside anything Wagic's DSL is meant to express: **Contraptions**
(the assemble-and-crank sprocket subgame), **host / augment** (the "Half-X, Half-—" mix-and-match
creature halves and the bare `Robo-` / `Rhino-` / `Ninja-` augment pieces), **physical dexterity**
(Ol' Buzzbark's dice-drop, Bee-Bee Gun, Really Epic Punch, Thud-for-Duds), **out-of-game / meta**
(Urza, Academy Headmaster pulling live rulings off a webpage; Rules Lawyer), and pervasive **coin
flips / dice rolls**. None is a distinct, engine-legal paper card; they are counted once each as
OUT-OF-SCOPE and are **not** analysed card-by-card below.

The remaining **fifteen** sets are a much thinner, more familiar tail: three reprint "Masters"
products (IMA, A25, PUMA), three Duel Decks / Guild Kit / Signature Spellbook reprint products
(DDT, DDU, SS1, GK1), two HasCon promo drops (H17, HTR), one Commander product (C18) and one
supplemental multiplayer set (BBD), plus the era's two "real" expansions **DOM (Dominaria)** and
**GRN (Guilds of Ravnica)**. Between them they contribute **60 distinct non-UST names**, of which
**one is STALE** and **three are reprint overlaps** — leaving **59 genuinely excluded** non-UST
cards. As in every prior era, 100% of the non-UST excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt`
(catalogued with full oracle text, grade *unsupported* → does not load, not AI-deck safe); none is
a dangling reference outside the catalogue.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### O1 — Unstable silver-border joke set  · OUT-OF-SCOPE  (191)

Every UST card. Not distinct engine-legal paper cards; grouped, not itemised. Sub-families:

- **Contraptions** (assemble/crank the sprocket subgame) — Steamflogger Service Rep, Rapid
  Prototyper, Widget Contraption, Cogmentor, Steamfloggery, Steamflogger of the Month,
  Steamflogger Temp, Wrench-Rigger, Joyride Rigger, Riveting Rigger, and every card that reads
  "assemble a Contraption" / "crank".
- **Host / augment** — the "Half-X, Half-—" host creatures (Half-Shark Half-—, Half-Squirrel
  Half-—, Half-Kitten Half-—, Half-Orc Half-—) and the bare augment pieces (`Robo-`,
  `Rhino-`, `Monkey-`, `Humming-`, `Ninja`, `Multi-Headed`, `Three-Headed Goblin`, `Serpentine`).
- **Physical dexterity / real-world action** — Ol' Buzzbark (drop dice), Bee-Bee Gun, Really
  Epic Punch, Thud-for-Duds, Hammerfest Boomtacular, Gnomeball Machine.
- **Out-of-game / meta / rules-text** — Urza, Academy Headmaster (live web rulings), Rules
  Lawyer, Ineffable Blessing (a–f), Very Cryptic Command (a–f), Everythingamajig (a–f),
  Knight of the Kitchen Sink (a–f), Garbage Elemental (a–f), Sly Spy (a–e) (per-printing variants).
- **Coin flips / dice / counting words / letters** — Baron Von Count, Clock of DOOOOOOOOOOOOM!,
  Magic Word, Staff of the Letter Magus, capital offense, X.

*(These do not map to Wagic's DSL and are excluded categorically. No further per-card work.)*

### E1 — Copy a triggered or activated ability  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

No primitive can copy an ability on the stack (Strionic Resonator itself, Rings of Brighthearth,
Illusionist's Bracers, Panharmonicon all unsupported; only Lithoform Engine is borderline). This
is distinct from copying a *spell* (which is supported for a single target).

- Strionic Resonator (A25) — {2},{T}: copy target triggered ability you control, may choose new targets
- Tawnos, Urza's Apprentice (C18) — {U}{R},{T}: copy target activated/triggered ability from an artifact source
- Bonus Round (BBD) — until EOT, each instant/sorcery a player casts is copied by its controller

### E2 — Repeated coin-flip loop ("flip a coin until you lose")  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

A single coin flip is supported (Frenetic Efreet), but the repeat-until-you-lose loop that drives
these Battlebond partners has no analog (Krark's Thumb / Fiery Gambit / Squee's Revenge all
unsupported). Their partner-with and the payoff (double P/T, draw) are supported; the loop is the wall.

- Okaun, Eye of Chaos (BBD) — combat: flip until you lose; each win doubles its P/T until EOT
- Zndrsplt, Eye of Wisdom (BBD) — combat: flip until you lose; each win draws a card

### E3 — Join forces (each player pays any amount of mana)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

The "starting with you, each player may pay any amount of mana, X = total" table-wide auction has
no primitive (Alliance of Arms, Collective Voyage both unsupported).

- Minds Aglow (CM2) — join forces; each player draws X
- Shared Trauma (CM2) — join forces; each player mills X

### E4 — Splice onto Arcane  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Both cards' *bodies* (put a creature into play, haste, sac / exile at end) are authorable —
Sneak Attack, Elvish Piper, Goryo's-style reanimate are all supported — but the **Splice onto
Arcane** alternative-cost mechanic that lets them ride an Arcane spell is unsupported (Glacial Ray,
Desperate Ritual unsupported). Filed at the splice blocker.

- Through the Breach (PUMA) — put a creature from hand into play w/ haste, sac EOT; Splice onto Arcane {2}{R}{R}
- Goryo's Vengeance (PUMA) — reanimate a legendary creature w/ haste, exile EOT; Splice onto Arcane {2}{B}

### E5 — Cipher (encode on a creature, recast on combat damage)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

The encode-on-a-creature-then-recast-free-on-combat-damage mechanic is unsupported (Hidden
Strings, Whispering Madness both unsupported). The spells' base effects (copy a permanent /
make a token) are trivial; cipher is the wall.

- Stolen Identity (GK1) — token copy of an artifact/creature; **Cipher**
- Call of the Nightwing (GK1) — make a 1/1 flying Horror; **Cipher**

### E6 — Change the target of a spell or ability (Willbender effect)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

No primitive can retarget an object already on the stack — the single largest recurring engine gap
across every era. All probed examples unsupported.

- Willbender (A25) — morph; face-up: change target of a single-target spell/ability *(reprint; see 2014–2016 doc)*
- Emissary of Grudges (C18) — reveal a secretly-chosen opponent: retarget their spell/ability aimed at you
- Razia, Boros Archangel (GK1) — {T}: redirect the next 3 damage to a creature you control onto another creature

### E7 — Fact-or-Fiction two-pile (opponent partitions, you keep one)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Opponent-splits-top-N-into-two-piles has no partition UI (carried over from prior eras).

- Sphinx of Uthuun (IMA, C18) — ETB reveal top 5, opponent splits into two piles, one to hand one to GY

### E8 — Copy a spell "for each other target" (Zada family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Copy-per-legal-target fan-out has no analog (single-target copy is supported).

- Zada, Hedron Grinder (A25) — a spell targeting only Zada is copied for each other creature you control

### E9 — Board-wide "each other creature becomes a copy"  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Base "enters as a copy" is supported (Clone); a live board-wide or repeatable become-a-copy is not
(Mirrorweave, Vesuvan Shapeshifter, Infinite Reflection all unsupported).

- Mirrorweave (CM2) — each other creature becomes a copy of target nonlegendary creature until EOT
- Vesuvan Shapeshifter (A25) — morph; on ETB/turn-face-up, become a copy of a chosen creature with a re-flip clause

### E10 — Damage-doubling replacement  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

"A source deals double that damage instead" is a per-source damage replacement the primitives
can't express (Furnace of Rath is only a borderline global; these one-sided/targeted variants
unsupported). Carried over from the 2014–2016 doc.

- Gisela, Blade of Goldnight (A25, CM2) — double damage to opponents, halve damage to you
- Bitter Feud (CM2) — chosen player deals double damage to the other chosen player

### E11 — Divide damage among any number of targets by turn-history maximum  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Needs both a free-form damage-division UI and a "greatest damage dealt this turn" turn-history read
(Impact Resonance unsupported; carried over from prior era).

- Impact Resonance (CM2) — X damage divided among any # of creatures, X = greatest damage dealt this turn

### E12 — Shared-target destruction/burn (you and an opponent each pick)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"…and target X of an opponent's choice" needs a second, opponent-chosen target on the same spell
(Volcanic Offering unsupported; carried over).

- Volcanic Offering (CM2) — destroy 2 nonbasic lands (one opp-chosen) + 7 damage to 2 creatures (one opp-chosen)

### E13 — Grant an alternative-cost keyword to every spell you cast  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Replicate as a base spell is supported; *granting* it (with a per-cast copy loop) to all your
instants/sorceries is a static-that-rewrites-every-spell the DSL can't currently scope.

- Djinn Illuminatus (GK1) — each instant/sorcery you cast has replicate = its mana cost

### E14 — Repeatable "become a copy of a card in an opponent's graveyard"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

One-shot become-a-copy-of-a-GY-card is supported (Body Double, Dimir Doppelganger); the standing
trigger that fires on every creature entering an opponent's GY, keeping the name/hexproof/ability,
is the rider.

- Lazav, Dimir Mastermind (GK1) — whenever a creature card hits an opp GY, may become a copy of it (name stays Lazav)

### E15 — Manifest-variant (end step, or exile-pile manifest)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Base manifest is supported (Rageform); these move the manifest source to an end-step trigger or an
exile-shuffle pile, plus a "play cards from a face-up exile" rider — none of which the primitive
wires up (Ghastly Conscription, Write into Being unsupported).

- Primordial Mist (C18) — end step: manifest top card; exile-a-face-down: play that card this turn
- Jeskai Infiltrator (C18) — combat damage: exile it + top card, shuffle, manifest the pile
- Reality Scramble (C18) — permanent to bottom, dig for a shared-card-type into play; **Retrace** *(retrace itself supported; the reveal-until-shared-type-into-play is the blocker)*

### E16 — Exile-N-then-cast-one-of-each-card-type-free  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"For each card type, you may cast a spell of that type from among the exiled cards for free" needs a
per-type cast-from-exile loop (Epic Experiment unsupported; Enter the Infinite's simpler draw is supported).

- Aminatou's Augury (C18) — exile top 8; play a land + one free spell of each nonland card type from them
- Gifts Ungiven (SS1) — search 4 differently-named cards; **opponent** chooses 2 to GY, rest to hand *(the opponent-partition is the true blocker → borders ENGINE-BLOCKED, filed MEDIUM as the pool is bounded)*

### E17 — Twin static cast-restrictions (CMC-lock + X-lock)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Single cast-restriction statics are supported (Void Winnower, Thalia, Trinisphere); Gaddock stacks
two clauses (CMC≥4 noncreature *and* any {X}-cost noncreature) — authorable but a careful two-static build.

- Gaddock Teeg (PUMA) — noncreature CMC≥4 can't be cast; noncreature with {X} can't be cast

### E18 — Reveal-top, cast if a numeric condition holds  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Reveal-top-then-cast-free is supported (Melek, Etali); Yennett gates it on a **CMC-parity** check
(cast only if odd, else draw) — a MEDIUM conditional on an otherwise-supported frame.

- Yennett, Cryptic Sovereign (C18) — attack: reveal top; cast free if CMC odd, else draw

### E19 — Gain control of attached Auras/Equipment and re-attach on bounce  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Bouncing a creature is trivial; the "gain control of all Auras/Equipment that were on it, then
attach them to another creature" is a multi-object re-attach the primitive doesn't sequence.

- Fumble (BBD) — bounce a creature; steal its Auras/Equipment and move them to another creature

### E20 — Counter-multiplier replacement (team-wide)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Doubling-Season-class counter multipliers are supported (Doubling Season, Corpsejack, Hardened
Scales all borderline); Pir adds a **team** scope and "+1 of each kind" wording — a MEDIUM variant.

- Pir, Imaginative Rascal (BBD) — partner; +1 extra of each kind of counter put on your team's permanents

### E21 — Fight-granting board aura + leaves-play draw payoff  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Grants every other creature an optional "fight me on attack" ability and, on leaving, draws for
each player equal to damage their sources dealt it — a damage-attribution tally the DSL can't track.

- Grothama, All-Devouring (BBD) — grant all creatures "may fight Grothama on attack"; LTB → draw = damage dealt to it per source-controller

### E22 — "Next time you would lose, instead…" life-total replacement  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

A one-shot replacement of a loss-the-game event with draw-7 + set-life-to-1, then self-exile.

- Stunning Reversal (BBD) — the next time you'd lose this turn, instead draw 7 and life becomes 1; exile it

### E23 — Blanket counter-removal + can't-get-counters lock  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Remove all counters from a target (or a player), then a standing "can't have/get counters while
this is on the battlefield" static — a modal ETB plus a counter-prevention replacement.

- Suncleanser (M19) — modal ETB: strip a creature's counters (+ lock), or strip a player's counters (+ lock)
- Amulet of Safekeeping (M19) — tax spells/abilities that target you {1}; creature tokens get -1/-0 *(the token-anthem debuff + targeting tax; MEDIUM)*

### E24 — Conditional-sacrifice tied to a counter on another permanent  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Put a "phylactery" marker on an artifact on ETB, grant indestructible, and sacrifice when no
permanent carries that marker — a cross-permanent counter dependency.

- Phylactery Lich (M19) — ETB put a phylactery counter on an artifact; indestructible; sac when none remain

### E25 — Trigger on opponent's specific action  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"Whenever an opponent activates a non-mana ability of a creature/land" and "whenever a player who
discarded may search a basic" — both are triggers on a narrowly-scoped opponent action with a payoff.

- Runic Armasaur (M19) — opp activates a non-mana creature/land ability → may draw
- Borderland Explorer (C18) — each player may discard; each who did may fetch a basic to hand

### E26 — Aura-count scaling payoff  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

"+1/+1 for each Aura you control attached to a creature" plus an ETB draw for the same count —
an Aura-population scan (carried over from the 2014–2016 doc; still unsupported).

- Sage's Reverie (C18) — ETB draw per attached Aura; enchanted creature +1/+1 per attached Aura

### E27 — Play cards from an opponent's graveyard for a limited window  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"Exile an opponent's GY; you may cast those cards this turn, any color of mana" — a per-card
cross-zone cast permission scoped to a turn (carried over from prior era's Hedonist's Trove family).

- Mnemonic Betrayal (GRN) — exile all opp GYs; cast from them this turn; return leftovers EOT
- Thief of Sanity (GRN) — combat damage: exile 1 of opp's top 3 face down; cast it later, any mana *(the standing cast-from-exile permission is the blocker; combat-damage trigger itself is fine)*

### E28 — Reveal-top-N, each opponent drafts one  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Multiplayer "starting with the next opponent, each chooses a different nonland card" — an
opponent-choice loop over a revealed pile (Manifold Insights unsupported).

- Manifold Insights (CM2) — reveal top 10; each opponent picks a different nonland to your hand

### E29 — Counters-persist-across-zones rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

"+1/+1 counters remain on it as it changes zones (except hand/library)" needs counter state to
survive a zone change the engine normally wipes (Skullbriar unsupported).

- Skullbriar, the Walking Grave (CM2) — combat damage → +1/+1; counters persist across zones

### E30 — Self-recursion: conditional-ETB counters + dies→suspend loop  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Conditional-ETB counters (if not hardcast) plus a dies→exile-with-time-counters-gains-suspend loop
(carried over from the 2014–2016 doc; Epochrasite unsupported).

- Epochrasite (CM2) — enters with three +1/+1 if not cast from hand; dies → suspend-3 self-recursion

### E31 — Master of the Wild Hunt combat resolution  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Wolf-token generation is trivial; the tap-all-Wolves-deal-power-then-that-creature-deals-back-power-
divided-among-them is a divided-damage exchange the DSL doesn't sequence.

- Master of the Wild Hunt (A25) — {T}: all your Wolves deal power to a creature; it deals power divided among them

### E32 — Karona's Zealot damage-redirection morph  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Morph + turn-face-up are supported; the face-up "all damage that would be dealt to it this turn is
dealt to target creature instead" is a per-turn redirect shield (redirect family broadly unsupported).

- Karona's Zealot (A25) — morph; face-up: redirect all damage that would hit it to a chosen creature

### E33 — Put a creature into play, sac unless you pay a reduced cost  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

The Sneak-Attack frame (into play, sac at end) is supported; Flash's twist is "sac it *unless you
pay its mana cost reduced by up to {2}*" — a pay-reduced-or-sacrifice choice on ETB.

- Flash (A25) — put a creature from hand into play; sac unless you pay its cost minus up to {2}

### E34 — HasCon planeswalker with dice-roll loyalty  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / OUT-OF-SCOPE  (1)

Dungeon Master's loyalty abilities lean on d20 / d4 rolls and a bespoke "adventuring party" token
suite; loyalty abilities themselves are supported but the die-roll outcomes and party ult put it
on the OUT-OF-SCOPE / MEDIUM boundary. Filed MEDIUM-with-caveat.

## Confidence & caveats

- **UST dominates the raw count and is entirely OUT-OF-SCOPE.** 191 of 251 distinct missing cards
  (76%) are Unstable silver-border designs — Contraptions, host/augment, physical dexterity,
  out-of-game meta, and coin/dice — none of which the DSL is meant to express. Excluding UST, the
  era's "real" gap is only **59 non-UST cards**, the smallest of any multi-set era so far, because
  the two actual expansions (DOM, GRN) are almost fully implemented.
- **Dominaria and Guilds of Ravnica are near-complete.** Every DOM marquee mechanic (Saga, kicker,
  historic-matters) and every GRN marquee mechanic (convoke, mentor, jump-start, undergrowth,
  surveil) has supported/borderline exemplars in `grade_index.json`. DOM contributes only **2**
  missing cards and GRN only **2** — both blocked by non-marquee riders (dual statics /
  cast-from-opponent-graveyard), not by the set's headline keyword.
- **The reprint products behave as expected.** IMA/A25/PUMA/DDT/DDU/SS1/GK1 missing-lists are small
  and are old-frame reprints of designs already flagged in earlier era docs — Willbender (retarget),
  Zada (copy-each-target), Sphinx of Uthuun (two-pile), Gisela (damage-doubling), Mirrorweave /
  Vesuvan Shapeshifter (mass-copy), Goblin Charbelcher (reveal-until-land). No reprint set introduces
  a new mechanic; their blockers are unchanged from prior audits.
- **One card is STALE and should be pulled from the missing-lists:** Oreskos Explorer (CM2/BBD)
  resolves to `borderline.txt` and loads today — the same STALE finding logged in the 2014–2016 doc
  (where it appeared under C15/PZ1).
- **Genuinely engine-blocked patterns confirmed by absence of any supported example:** copy-a-
  triggered/activated-ability (Strionic Resonator, Tawnos, Bonus Round — Rings of Brighthearth /
  Illusionist's Bracers / Panharmonicon all unsupported), the **flip-a-coin-until-you-lose** loop
  (Okaun, Zndrsplt), **join forces** (Minds Aglow, Shared Trauma), **splice onto Arcane** (Through
  the Breach, Goryo's Vengeance), **cipher** (Stolen Identity, Call of the Nightwing), and the
  perennial **change-target / Fact-or-Fiction / copy-for-each-target / board-wide-copy / damage-
  doubling** families carried over from earlier eras.
- **No BACKLOG-EASY card in this era.** The two novelty singletons that might have looked easy
  (Dungeon Master, Nerf War) are pulled toward OUT-OF-SCOPE by their die-roll and physical-action
  clauses; every other non-UST card needs at least a careful multi-clause build (MEDIUM) or is
  ENGINE-BLOCKED.
- 100% of the 59 non-UST excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — this audit measures the
  *documented* unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set
  (everything Wagic *does* implement) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (Gifts Ungiven's opponent-partition, Reality Scramble's reveal-until, the splice bodies
  whose non-splice halves are authorable). Each is counted once, at its hardest blocker.
