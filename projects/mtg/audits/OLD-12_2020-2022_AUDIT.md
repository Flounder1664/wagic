# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2020–2022 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**ZNC · PLIST · KLR · CMR · KHM · HA4 · TSR · C21 · STA · HA5 · MH2 · MIC · MID · Q06 · VOC · DBL**
(Zendikar Rising Commander, The List, Kaladesh Remastered, Commander Legends, Kaldheim,
Historic Anthology 4, Time Spiral Remastered, Commander 2021, Strixhaven Mystical Archive,
Historic Anthology 5, Modern Horizons 2, Midnight Hunt Commander, Innistrad: Midnight Hunt,
Alchemy: Innistrad [Q06], Crimson Vow Commander, Innistrad Double Feature).

Generated 2026-07-11. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the peer [`OLD-08_2014-2016_AUDIT.md`](OLD-08_2014-2016_AUDIT.md).
Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index
to confirm no supported/borderline example exists.

Of **82** raw `[card]` blocks across the 16 files, dedup collapses to **74 distinct names**
(6 names appear in two sets, 1 name in three sets — see reprint table below). Three of those
already resolve to a `borderline` primitive and **should be dropped from the missing-lists** — they
silently work today. The remaining **71** are all catalogued-unsupported: upstream registered them
with full oracle text inside `sets/primitives/unsupported.txt` (grade *unsupported* → does not load,
not AI-deck safe) but never wrote a working primitive. None are true dangling references outside the
catalogue. So the excluded population is "known-broken, documented" — the same shape as every prior
era.

Note this era's missing-lists are **dominated by reprint products** (PLIST = The List, KLR = Kaladesh
Remastered, TSR = Time Spiral Remastered, STA = Strixhaven Mystical Archive, and the four Commander
decks ZNC/C21/MIC/VOC). Because these products reprint older designs, the missing cards are almost
entirely **old engine-blocked mechanics resurfacing** (voting, cipher, clash, coin flip, ripple/
replicate, copy-a-spell-retarget) rather than genuinely new-set gaps. The set-specific new mechanics
of the era — **snow, foretell, boast, saga (KHM); daybound/nightbound, disturb, decayed, flashback
(MID/MIC/VOC/DBL)** — are all **supported** (see probes), so those blocks are blocked by *other*
clauses, never the headline keyword.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Will of the council (voting)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

No primitive runs a starting-with-you player vote and resolves the majority. Every voting card in
the catalogue is unsupported (Plea for Power, Expropriate, Coercive Portal, Capital Punishment,
Split Decision); only Custodi Lich squeaks to borderline by ignoring the vote UI. This is the
signature engine gap of the Commander-heavy era.

- Council's Judgment (PLIST) — each player votes for a nonland permanent you don't control; exile the winner(s)
- Magister of Worth (C21) — vote grace (mass-reanimate) vs. condemnation (board wipe)
- Custodi Squire (VOC) — vote for a card in your graveyard; return the winner(s) to hand

### E2 — Cipher  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

"Exile this spell encoded on a creature; recast a copy on combat damage" — no encode-on-creature
zone exists. All cipher cards unsupported.

- Stolen Identity (ZNC) — token-copy artifact/creature; cipher
- Hidden Strings (Q06) — tap/untap two permanents; cipher
- (both blocked identically; Stolen Identity also carries an E5 copy clause)

### E3 — Copy a spell, then change or reselect its targets  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

Single-target copy keeps its target (Twincast/Fork supported), but "copy, then reselect/randomize
targets" and "gain control of the spell, then copy" have no analog. All unsupported.

- Stolen Identity (ZNC) — *(token-copy of a permanent; grouped here for its copy body — primary blocker cipher, E2)*
- Chef's Kiss (MH2) — gain control of a single-target spell, copy it, reselect both targets at random
- Yusri, Fortune's Flame (MH2) — *(coin-flip payoff; filed E9)*

*(Chef's Kiss is the true member; it fuses gain-control-of-spell (E6-class) with random retarget.)*

### E4 — Copy a spell/ability "for each other legal target" (Precursor/Zada family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Copy-per-legal-target fan-out; Precursor Golem and Zada are both unsupported.

- Radiant Performer (C21) — copy a single-target spell/ability for each other permanent/player it could target

### E5 — Change the target of a spell/ability ("Willbender effect")  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No primitive retargets an object already on the stack. Spellskite, Mizzium Meddler, Willbender all
unsupported; only fixed "Redirect" is borderline.

- Muck Drubb (TSR) — ETB: change the target of a single-creature-targeting spell to Muck Drubb (also has madness, supported)

### E6 — Copy an activated ability (pay-to-copy)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

"Whenever you activate an ability, pay {2}: copy it." Rings of Brighthearth, Strionic Resonator,
Illusionist's Bracers all unsupported — no ability-copy hook.

- Rings of Brighthearth (CMR) — copy a non-mana activated ability for {2}
- Battlemage's Bracers (C21) — equipped creature's abilities can be copied for {1} (also grants haste)

### E7 — Additional-trigger doubler (Panharmonicon)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"ETB/that trigger triggers an additional time" — no trigger-count multiplier. Panharmonicon, Yarok,
Elesh Norn (MoM) all unsupported.

- Panharmonicon (PLIST, KLR, TSR) — artifact/creature-ETB triggers fire an extra time

### E8 — Activated-ability cost reduction  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Blanket "activated abilities of your creatures cost {N} less" (with a floor). Training Grounds,
Heartstone, Zirda all unsupported.

- Training Grounds (PLIST) — your creatures' activated abilities cost up to {2} less

### E9 — Coin flip  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

No coin-flip primitive; every flip card unsupported (Karplusan Minotaur, Fiery Gambit, Goblin Bookie).

- Krark's Thumb (PLIST) — flip two coins, ignore one
- Yusri, Fortune's Flame (MH2) — attack: flip N coins, draw/lose per result, free spells on five wins
- (Chef's Kiss's random retarget is a different gap → E3)

### E10 — Clash  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Reveal-top, keep-or-bottom, compare-CMC "clash" has no UI. Lash Out and all clash cards unsupported.

- Lash Out (PLIST) — 3 damage to a creature; clash to also hit its controller

### E11 — Ripple / Replicate  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Cascade-adjacent "reveal N and free-cast same-named / copy-per-payment" fan-outs; both unsupported.

- Thrumming Stone (PLIST) — your spells have ripple 4
- Djinn Illuminatus (PLIST) — your instants/sorceries gain replicate

### E12 — Splice onto Arcane  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Reveal-from-hand-and-add-effects-to-an-Arcane-spell; the splice-graft has no primitive.

- Evermind (PLIST) — draw a card; splice onto Arcane {1}{U}

### E13 — Hideaway (library-size-gated free cast)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Exile-face-down-then-cast-when-a-condition-is-met. The land-count-gated Shelldock variant is
unsupported (the creature-attack-gated Windbrisk/Mosswort are only borderline).

- Shelldock Isle (PLIST) — hideaway; free-cast the hidden card when a library has ≤20 cards

### E14 — Improvise  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"Tap artifacts to pay {1} each" toward a nonartifact spell — no artifact-as-generic-mana hook.

- Inspiring Statuary (HA4) — your nonartifact spells have improvise

### E15 — Partial / choose-a-source / redirect prevention (en-Kor family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

`prevent:` is flat only; per-source redirect-to-another-creature all fail (Deflecting Palm,
Comeuppance, Outrider en-Kor confirmed unsupported).

- Deflecting Palm (CMR) — prevent damage from a chosen source, deal it back to that source's controller
- Kor Dirge (TSR) — redirect all damage from one of your creatures to another creature
- Outrider en-Kor (TSR) — {0}: redirect its next 1 damage onto a creature you control (also flanking, supported)

### E16 — Secret simultaneous number choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Every player picks a hidden number, reveal at once, compare. Menacing Ogre / Master of Predicaments
family — no hidden-numeric-choice UI.

- Wheel of Misfortune (CMR) — all players secretly choose a number; highest gets burned, non-lowest wheel

### E17 — Gain-control-of-a-spell / opponent-plays-under-you  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Seizing an object off the stack, or granting yourself permission to play an opponent's just-searched
cards, both lack a primitive (Commandeer/Aethersnatch unsupported; Opposition Agent's control-the-
searcher hijack is the same class of stack/zone takeover).

- Opposition Agent (CMR) — flash; you control opponents while they search, and may play what they find

### E18 — Saga clause bodies (frame supported, bodies not)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Saga chapters are supported (History of Benalia). Ascent is blocked by its I/II damage-redirect
(en-Kor-class) plus a III reanimate-with-flying-counter-and-type-change.

- Ascent of the Worthy (KHM) — I/II redirect all creature damage to one creature; III reanimate as flying Angel Warrior

### E19 — Sacrifice-N-creatures → P/T = their total (sum-on-cast/ETB)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

"Sacrifice any number of creatures; make/become an X/X where X = their total power." No sum-of-
sacrificed-power primitive (same gap the peer doc flagged for Dracoplasm / Stitcher Geralf).

- Corpse Cobble (MID, DBL) — sac creatures, make an X/X Zombie = total power; flashback (supported)
- Stitcher Geralf (MIC) — mill, exile ≤2, make an X/X Zombie = total power exiled
- Voracious Dragon (PLIST) — devour (supported); ETB deals damage = twice the Goblins devoured

*(All three need the same "total power of a sacrificed/exiled pile → dynamic value" plumbing.)*

### E20 — Counter-then-suspend / counter-then-copy riders  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Suspend is fully supported (Aeon Chronicler, Ancestral Vision) and single-target copy is supported,
but bolting them onto a counter is the gap.

- Delay (TSR) — counter a spell, then exile it with 3 time counters and grant suspend
- Wildfire Devils (TSR-adjacent: C21) — random player exiles an instant/sorcery from GY; copy and free-cast it

*(Wildfire Devils is really a copy-from-a-graveyard variant; grouped here for the "copy a
non-stack spell" shape. Filed MEDIUM.)*

### E21 — Reanimate/animate as an Aura with extra bookkeeping  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Aura-reanimation is supported (Animate Dead, Dance of the Dead, Necromancy-borderline). These two
add an exile-and-return-noting-counters or a licid transform-into-aura toggle the primitive lacks.

- Oubliette (PLIST) — exile a creature + its Auras, note counters; return them all when Oubliette leaves
- Calming Licid (PLIST) — {W},{T}: becomes an Aura attached to a creature; {W} to end (licid toggle)

### E22 — Reveal-and-partition / opponent-splits-into-two-piles  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Opponent partitions a revealed pile, you (or they) pick — no partition UI (peer-era Steam Augury /
Sphinx of Uthuun class).

- Sphinx of Uthuun (CMR) — ETB reveal top 5; opponent splits into two piles, one to hand, one to GY

### E23 — Counter target activated/triggered ability (standalone)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

The counter-an-ability machinery exists (Disallow, Voidslime, Summary Dismissal all borderline), so
Stifle's standalone version is authorable — it just wasn't written.

- Stifle (HA5) — counter target activated or triggered ability

### E24 — Choose-two modal instant  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Choose-two-of-four with a mix of prevent-spell-damage / sac-enchantment / +1+1 / fight modes
(peer doc filed this exact card MEDIUM under E41).

- Dromoka's Command (HA5) — choose two: prevent instant/sorcery damage · sac an enchantment · +1/+1 counter · fight

### E25 — Opponent-choice: draw vs. mill-and-burn  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

The "target opponent may have you draw instead" frame exists (Browbeat borderline; Book of Rass
supported); Combustible's blocker is the mill-three-then-deal-total-CMC dynamic damage.

- Combustible Gearhulk (KLR, C21) — opponent lets you draw 3, or mills your top 3 and takes damage = their total CMC

### E26 — Free-cast X-CMC-or-less spells from an exiled pile  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Exile top X, free-cast every instant/sorcery of CMC ≤ X (peer doc filed Epic Experiment MEDIUM under
"cast-multiple-from-exile").

- Epic Experiment (TSR, C21) — exile top X; free-cast each instant/sorcery of CMC ≤ X among them

### E27 — Name-a-card, search-all-zones, exile-and-refill  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Naming works (Nevermore/Memoricide borderline), but the search-graveyard-hand-library-exile-then-
draw-per-hand-card combo isn't wired.

- Lost Legacy (KLR) — name a card; exile all copies from a player's GY+hand+library; they draw per hand copy exiled

### E28 — Exile-top-and-continue-unless-duplicate (impulsive dig loop)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Repeat "exile top, keep unless name-collision" — a stateful loop over exiled names.

- Tainted Pact (STA) — exile top repeatedly; take one into hand, or stop at the first duplicate name

### E29 — Boast-style attack-triggered coin/random — n/a; see E9

*(No separate group; Yusri is filed under E9 coin flip.)*

### E30 — Exile-and-gain-all-activated-abilities  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

"This has all activated abilities of the creatures exiled with it" — needs to graft arbitrary
exiled-card abilities onto the host.

- Dark Impostor (VOC) — {4}{B}{B}: exile a creature + counter; gain all its activated abilities

### E31 — Grant madness to a card type  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Madness itself is supported (Fiery Temper); *granting* madness to every Vampire you own is the gap
(peer doc filed Falkenrath Gorger MEDIUM).

- Falkenrath Gorger (VOC) — every Vampire card you own gains madness = its mana cost

### E32 — Devour-count payoff — see E19

*(Voracious Dragon's devour is supported; its "twice the Goblins devoured" damage is the sum-payoff
gap, grouped with E19.)*

### E33 — Cascade rider (put a land into play as you cascade)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Cascade is supported (Bloodbraid Elf); the "you may put a land from the exiled cards into play" hook
mid-cascade is the gap.

- Averna, the Chaos Bloom (CMR) — as you cascade, put a revealed land onto the battlefield tapped

### E34 — Draw-replacement → make a Treasure  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Treasure tokens are supported (Brass's Bounty); the "if an opponent would draw an extra card,
instead you make a Treasure" replacement is the gap.

- Hullbreacher (CMR) — flash; opponents' extra draws become Treasures for you

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of the 71 excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — this audit measures the *documented*
  unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set (everything Wagic
  *does* implement) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope.
- The era's set-specific new mechanics are **overwhelmingly supported**: saga, snow, foretell, boast
  (KHM); daybound/nightbound, disturb, decayed, flashback (MID/VOW/commander decks); plus the older
  suspend, echo, flanking, madness, cascade, devour, treasure, goad reprinted in TSR/PLIST/CMR all
  have supported/borderline exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is why the set-specific missing
  count is tiny (KHM 1, MID 1, STA 1) and most excluded cards land in BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by a
  rider") or are old engine-blocked designs resurfacing through reprint products.
- **Three cards are STALE and should be pulled from the missing-lists**: Necromancy (PLIST), Oreskos
  Explorer (CMR/C21), and Secret Plans (TSR) — all resolve to `borderline.txt` and load today.
- The **signature engine gap of this era is voting (will of the council)** — Council's Judgment,
  Magister of Worth, Custodi Squire — driven by the four Commander products. No primitive runs a
  starting-with-you vote; every voting card in the catalogue is unsupported except Custodi Lich
  (borderline, ignores the vote UI).
- Other genuinely engine-blocked patterns confirmed by absence of any supported example: **cipher**
  (Stolen Identity, Hidden Strings), **clash** (Lash Out), **coin flip** (Krark's Thumb, Yusri),
  **ripple/replicate** (Thrumming Stone, Djinn Illuminatus), **splice onto Arcane** (Evermind),
  **improvise** (Inspiring Statuary), **copy-an-ability** (Rings of Brighthearth, Battlemage's
  Bracers), **additional-trigger doubler** (Panharmonicon), **activated-ability cost reduction**
  (Training Grounds), **change/reselect a spell's target** (Muck Drubb, Chef's Kiss, Radiant
  Performer), **en-Kor redirect prevention** (Deflecting Palm, Kor Dirge, Outrider en-Kor), and
  **secret number choice** (Wheel of Misfortune).
- **No OUT-OF-SCOPE bucket** surfaces: despite three Arena/Alchemy products in the run (HA4, HA5,
  Q06), every one of their missing entries is an ordinary paper reprint (Inspiring Statuary;
  Dromoka's Command, Stifle; Hidden Strings), not a digital-only rebalance. MH2's Yusri and Chef's
  Kiss are paper. And no Planechase Plane cards appear.
- **CMR (Commander Legends)** — notably, its missing-list contains **no partner or draft-matters
  card**; every entry is an ordinary Commander-style card. The partner/draft-matters subsystem gap
  the prompt flagged does **not** surface in `missing_cards_by_sets/CMR.txt`.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). No card reached BACKLOG-EASY this era — even the simplest excluded cards
  (Scroll Rack, Muraganda Petroglyphs) carry a rider that lifts them to MEDIUM.
