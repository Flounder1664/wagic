# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2014–2016 (16 sets)

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**M15 · DDN · KTK · C14 · JVC · FRF · DTK · TPR · MM2 · ORI · BFZ · C15 · PZ1 · OGW · SOI · EMA**
(Magic 2015, Duel Decks: Speed vs. Cunning, Khans of Tarkir, Commander 2014, Duel Decks:
Jace vs. Vraska, Fate Reforged, Dragons of Tarkir, Tempest Remastered, Modern Masters 2015,
Magic Origins, Battle for Zendikar, Commander 2015, Planechase Anthology, Oath of the Gatewatch,
Shadows over Innistrad, Eternal Masters).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the SOS backlog. Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the
underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no supported example exists.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — "Change the target of a spell or ability" (Willbender effect)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (6)

No primitive can retarget an object already on the stack (or an ability). Every probed example —
Spellskite, Mizzium Meddler, Silver Wyvern, Willbender itself — is unsupported; only the fixed
"Redirect" spell is even borderline. This is the single largest engine-blocked group in the era,
inflated by Willbender's three reprints.

- Willbender (DDN, C14, JVC) — morph; face-up: change target of a single-target spell/ability
- Quicksilver Dragon (JVC) — {U}: retarget a spell aimed only at it, to another creature
- Silver Wyvern (TPR) — {U}: retarget a spell/ability aimed only at it, to a creature
- Spellskite (MM2) — {PU}: change a target of target spell/ability to Spellskite
- Mizzium Meddler (ORI) — ETB: may change a target of target spell/ability to it
- Kheru Spellsnatcher (KTK) — *counter*-then-may-cast rider (also E-counter family; primary blocker is the face-up counter-exile-cast, filed E12)

### E2 — Copy a spell "for each other target" (Precursor / Zada family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Single-target copy is supported (Twincast/Fork), but "copy the spell once for each other Golem/creature it could target, each copy a different one" needs a copy-per-legal-target fan-out the engine has no analog for.

- Precursor Golem (MM2) — any spell targeting a single Golem is copied for each other Golem
- Zada, Hedron Grinder (BFZ) — spell targeting only Zada copied for each other creature you control

### E3 — Copy / mass-copy a creature (repeatable, revealed, or board-wide)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Base "enters as a copy" is supported (Clone); these need a live board-wide or top-of-library copy.

- Infinite Reflection (C14) — every other nontoken creature you control becomes a copy of the enchanted creature (and future ones enter as copies)
- Deceiver of Form (OGW) — combat: reveal top card, other creatures you control may become copies of it until EOT

### E4 — Gain control of a spell on the stack  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No primitive to seize an object off the stack (Commandeer/Aethersnatch both unsupported).

- Aethersnatch (C15, PZ1) — gain control of target spell, may choose new targets

### E5 — Counter a spell and put it onto the battlefield under your control  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Counter-target-spell is supported, but the "instead put that artifact/creature into play under your control" replacement is not (Gilded Drake-style; Desertion unsupported).

- Desertion (PZ1) — counter target spell; steal a countered artifact/creature spell into play

### E6 — Fact-or-Fiction two-pile (opponent partitions, you choose)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (4)

Opponent-splits-into-two-piles-you-pick has no partition UI (even Fact or Fiction is only borderline; every reveal-5-into-two-piles card here is unsupported).

- Steam Augury (DDN, C15) — reveal top 5, split into two piles, opponent chooses which you keep
- Sphinx of Uthuun (DDN, C14) — ETB reveal top 5, opponent splits into two piles
- Epiphany at the Drownyard (SOI) — reveal top X+1, split into two piles, opponent chooses
- (Steam Augury / Epiphany share the "you split, opponent chooses" wording; Sphinx is opponent-splits — both fail on the same missing partition machinery)

### E7 — Partial / choose-a-source / redirect prevention shields  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (10)

Wagic's `prevent:` is only a flat "prevent all/next-N"; per-source, choose-a-source, redirect-to-a-creature, and "convert prevented damage into a new effect" all fail. Confirmed: none of the redirect examples are supported.

- Deflecting Palm (KTK) — prevent damage from a chosen source, deal it back to that source's controller
- Comeuppance (C14) — prevent all damage to you from sources you don't control, reflect it
- Channel Harm (FRF) — prevent all damage from sources you don't control, redirect to a creature
- Kor Chant (TPR) — redirect all damage from one of your creatures to another creature
- Spirit en-Kor (TPR) — {0}: redirect the next 1 damage to it onto a creature you control
- Warrior en-Kor (TPR) — {0}: same redirect-1 effect
- Nomads en-Kor (TPR) — {0}: same redirect-1 effect
- Shaman en-Kor (TPR) — {0}: redirect-1, plus a choose-a-source redirect-to-itself ability
- Soltari Guerrillas (TPR) — {0}: redirect its next combat damage to a creature
- (Gisela's "prevent half to you" and Bitter Feud's doubling are filed under E8, not here)

### E8 — Damage-doubling replacement (deal double / take double)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

"If a source would deal damage … it deals double instead" is a damage replacement the primitives can't express per-source (Furnace of Rath is only a borderline global; these targeted/one-sided variants are unsupported).

- Gisela, Blade of Goldnight (C15) — double damage to opponents, halve damage to you
- Bitter Feud (C14) — chosen player deals double damage to the other chosen player
- Might Makes Right (M15) — control-swap engine keyed on "greatest power" (files here for its combat-begin control theft; see also E17) — *(reassigned: see E17)*

*(Might Makes Right's real blocker is the "you control each greatest-power creature" gate → E17; only Gisela and Bitter Feud are true damage-doublers → this group is effectively 2.)*

### E9 — Exchange control of two permanents  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Two-way control swap of two chosen permanents sharing a type (all probed unsupported).

- Legerdemain (TPR) — swap control of an artifact/creature and a permanent sharing its type
- Shifting Loyalties (FRF) — exchange control of two permanents that share a card type

### E10 — Guess / hidden-numeric choice  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Simultaneous secret/guess mechanics have no digital analog (Menacing Ogre family).

- Master of Predicaments (M15) — opponent guesses if a hidden card's CMC > 4; if wrong you cast it free

### E11 — "End the turn" (turn truncation)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Exile-everything-and-skip-to-cleanup is a turn-structure operation the engine can't perform (Time Stop / Sundial equally unsupported).

- Day's Undoing (ORI) — each player shuffles hand+GY, draws 7; if your turn, end the turn

### E12 — Face-up "counter target spell, then may cast it" rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Base morph + turn-face-up trigger are supported; the counter-then-exile-then-cast-for-free chain is the blocker.

- Kheru Spellsnatcher (KTK) — face-up: counter a spell, exile it, may cast it free while exiled

### E13 — Look-at-hidden-zone utility (opponent's face-down / top of library)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Passive "you may look at face-down creatures you don't control / the top of your library" — the hidden-information peek has no primitive, but it is a pure utility with no board effect. All three unsupported.

- Smoke Teller (KTK) — {1}{U}: look at a target face-down creature
- Lens of Clarity (KTK) — look at top of your library and face-down creatures you don't control
- Keeper of the Lens (DTK) — may look at face-down creatures you don't control

### E14 — Manifest-variant (from a graveyard, from top-of-library, or exile-and-manifest a pile)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

Base manifest is supported (Rageform); these move the manifest *source* to a graveyard pile, the library top at end step, or an exile-shuffle pile — none of which the primitive wires up.

- Ghastly Conscription (FRF) — exile all creature cards from a GY, shuffle, manifest them all
- Write into Being (FRF) — look at top 2, manifest one, other to top/bottom
- Whisperwood Elemental (FRF) — end step: manifest top card; sac to grant dies→manifest
- Jeskai Infiltrator (FRF) — combat damage: exile it + top card, shuffle, manifest the pile

### E15 — Choose-mode "Siege" — specific modes unsupported  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

The choose-Khans-or-Dragons ETB frame IS supported (Citadel Siege, Outpost Siege). These two are blocked by their specific mode bodies (repeating mana-add / ETB-fight-on-flyer / opponent-cost-increase).

- Frontier Siege (FRF) — Khans: add {G}{G} each main phase · Dragons: flyer ETB → fight
- Monastery Siege (FRF) — Khans: draw-then-discard each draw step · Dragons: tax opponent's targeting spells {2}

### E16 — Modal "choose one that hasn't been chosen" escalating upkeep  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Needs to remember which modes were already chosen across turns, including a "you lose the game" mode.

- Demonic Pact (ORI) — each upkeep pick an unused mode; the fourth is "you lose the game"

### E17 — Control-theft gated on a global board condition  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Temporary control theft is supported (Act of Treason-style), but these gate on a per-turn global check ("you control each greatest-power creature", pay-a-cost each combat) or reattach the theft aura on every opponent ETB.

- Might Makes Right (M15) — combat: if you control every greatest-power creature, steal one until EOT
- Yasova Dragonclaw (FRF) — combat: may pay {1}{UR}{UR} to steal a lower-power creature until EOT
- Illusory Gains (DTK) — control-aura that re-attaches to each creature an opponent brings in

### E18 — Give-control-away with an upkeep / repeatable rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Simple one-shot "target player gains control" works (Bazaar Trader), but these bundle a repeating upkeep hand-off, an equipment attach, or a loyalty-style cost.

- Crown of Doom (C14) — {2}: give it to another player; pump attackers against its owner
- Assault Suit (C14) — equipment; each opponent's upkeep you may hand them the creature untapped
- Zedruu the Greathearted (PZ1) — draw/gain per donated permanent; {R}{W}{U}: donate a permanent

### E19 — "The Chain Veil" loyalty-reactivation  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Needs to track per-turn loyalty-ability activation and grant extra activations across all your planeswalkers (loyalty abilities themselves are supported).

- The Chain Veil (M15) — end-step life-loss if no loyalty used; {4},{T}: re-enable one loyalty ability on each planeswalker

### E20 — Reanimate-into-play then exile on a later condition  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Reanimation is supported, but the deferred "exile that creature when I lose control / it untaps / an opponent chooses two survivors" bookkeeping is not.

- Coffin Queen (TPR) — reanimate a GY creature; exile it when Coffin Queen untaps or you lose control
- Archfiend of Depravity (FRF) — each opponent's end step, they keep two creatures and sacrifice the rest

### E21 — Reveal-until-creature-into-play (Oath of Druids)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Reveal-from-top-until-a-type-then-put-into-play with an opponent-count gate.

- Oath of Druids (TPR) — upkeep: if an opponent has more creatures, dig for a creature into play

### E22 — Exiled-card-becomes-body / cast-on-death  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Exile a spell card, make the creature's P/T equal its CMC, then cast that card free on death.

- Living Lore (DTK) — ETB exile an instant/sorcery; P/T = its CMC; sac on combat damage to cast it

### E23 — Play cards from a special exile zone  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"You may play the cards exiled this way" (from an opponent's GY or their library) — a per-card cross-zone cast permission the primitives can't scope.

- Hedonist's Trove (DTK) — exile an opponent's GY; play lands freely, one spell per turn from it
- Stolen Goods (C15) — mill an opponent to the first nonland; you may cast it this turn

### E24 — Grant / scale madness  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Madness itself is supported (Fiery Temper), but *granting* madness to a whole card type, or an X-scaled madness cost, is not.

- Falkenrath Gorger (SOI) — every Vampire card you own gains madness = its mana cost
- Welcome to the Fold (SOI) — has madness {X}{U}{U}; the X-value changes the control clause's threshold

### E25 — Dynamic name-lock (capture a name at resolution, then forbid casting it)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Naming a *pre-chosen* card to lock it works (Nevermore/Meddling Mage borderline). These capture the name dynamically — from a revealed hand or from a bounced creature — which the primitive can't.

- Alhammarret, High Arbiter (ORI) — reveal opponents' hands, you name a card, they can't cast it
- Reflector Mage (OGW) — bounce a creature; its owner can't recast that *name* until your next turn

### E26 — Sacrifice-N to define P/T on entry  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

"As it enters, sacrifice any number of creatures; its P/T become their totals." No sum-on-ETB primitive.

- Dracoplasm (TPR) — ETB sacrifice creatures; P/T become their total power / total toughness

### E27 — Remove-all-counters-then-scale  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Remove every counter from every permanent and convert the total into +1/+1 counters on itself.

- Thief of Blood (C15, PZ1) — ETB strip all counters everywhere; enters with that many +1/+1

### E28 — Remove +1/+1 counters as a cost to shrink  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Counter-as-cost feeding a -X/-X where X = counters removed (Retribution unsupported; the counter-removal-cost + scaled debuff combo is the gap).

- Retribution of the Ancients (KTK) — {B}, remove X counters from your creatures: target gets -X/-X

### E29 — Reveal a random card from hand → scaled effect  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Random selection from hand plus a CMC-scaled damage payoff.

- Friendly Fire (FRF) — target's controller reveals a random hand card; deal its CMC to creature+player

### E30 — Tap + "doesn't untap for as long as I control this"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Frost-Titan-style lock: tap a creature and keep it tapped tied to controlling the source (Frost Titan is only borderline; this variant unsupported).

- Icefall Regent (DTK) — ETB tap a creature; it stays tapped while you control Icefall Regent

### E31 — Turn-face-up rider on an otherwise-supported morph  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Morph/megamorph and the turn-face-up trigger are both supported; these two carry a specific face-up/death rider the primitive didn't author.

- Ashcloud Phoenix (KTK) — dies → return face down; face-up → 2 damage to each player
- Pine Walker (KTK) — face-up (of it or another creature) → untap that creature

### E32 — Count-Auras / Aura-scaling payoffs  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Aura-based scaling ("+1/+1 for each Aura", "draw per Aura", "+1/+1 per counter on OTHER creatures + grant an ability").

- Sage's Reverie (FRF) — ETB draw per Aura on creatures; enchanted creature +1/+1 per such Aura
- Gleam of Authority (DTK) — enchanted creature +1/+1 per +1/+1 counter on your other creatures; grants a bolster ability

### E33 — Delve-granted keyword transfer  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Delve is supported as a cost-reducer, but "if a creature with keyword X was delved away, this gains X" needs to inspect the exiled-for-delve cards' keywords.

- Soulflayer (FRF) — inherits flying/first strike/deathtouch/etc. from creatures exiled to its delve

### E34 — Imprint-land then count same-named lands  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Imprint a land on ETB, then a live global count of lands sharing its name driving an equip bonus.

- Strata Scythe (C14) — imprint a land; equipped creature +1/+1 per land of that name in play

### E35 — Whole-board shuffle-and-reset  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Warp-World-class: every player shuffles hand + all owned permanents into library and redraws that many (a large sequenced mass operation; Warp World itself is unsupported).

- The Great Aurora (ORI) — everyone shuffles hand+permanents into library, redraws that many, may play lands

### E36 — Copy-a-spell-then-mastery rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Copying a *countered* spell (not one on the stack) gated on a graveyard-count spell-mastery check.

- Psychic Rebuttal (ORI) — counter a spell targeting you; spell-mastery → copy the countered spell

### E37 — Turn-all-creatures-face-down + dynamic P/T  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Board-wide "turn every other creature face down" plus a self P/T equal to the face-down count.

- Ixidron (C14) — ETB flip all other nontoken creatures face down; its P/T = # face-down creatures

### E38 — Face-down / manifest support cards blocked by their static rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Cost-reducer / face-down anthem statics for morph decks; both unsupported despite morph itself working.

- Obscuring Aether (DTK) — face-down creature spells cost {1} less; {1}{G}: turn it face down
- Trail of Mystery (KTK) — face-down ETB → land tutor; turned-face-up creature gets +2/+2

### E39 — Extra-turn denial / take-an-extra-turn on death  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Static "extra turns are skipped" plus a dies-replacement that grants its controller an extra turn.

- Ugin's Nexus (KTK) — players skip extra turns; if it would die, exile it and take an extra turn

### E40 — Self-recursion: enter-with-counters-if-not-cast + dies-to-suspend  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Conditional-ETB counters (if not cast from hand) plus a dies→exile-with-time-counters-gains-suspend loop.

- Epochrasite (C14) — enters with three +1/+1 if not hardcast; dies → suspend-3 self-recursion

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of the 109 excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — this audit measures the
  *documented* unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set
  (everything Wagic *does* implement) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope.
- The era's marquee mechanics are **overwhelmingly supported**: morph/megamorph, manifest (base),
  delve, prowess, raid, dash, bolster, outlast, ferocious, devoid, ingest, awaken, converge, rally,
  support, cohort, surge, renown, spell-mastery, experience counters, madness, transform-DFC,
  delirium, investigate, skulk, fight, and the choose-mode Siege frame all have supported exemplars
  in `grade_index.json`. That is why most cards land in BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by a rider")
  rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED.
- **Three cards are STALE and should be pulled from the missing-lists**: Oreskos Explorer (C15/PZ1),
  Salt Road Ambushers (DTK), and Secret Plans (KTK) — all resolve to `borderline.txt` and load today.
- Genuinely engine-blocked patterns confirmed by absence of any supported example: **change the
  target of a spell/ability** (the "Willbender effect" — the single biggest engine group, six cards
  incl. Spellskite and Mizzium Meddler), **copy-a-spell-for-each-target** (Precursor Golem, Zada),
  **mass/repeatable creature-copy** (Infinite Reflection, Deceiver of Form), **gain-control-of-a-spell**
  (Aethersnatch), **counter-into-play** (Desertion), **Fact-or-Fiction two-pile** (Steam Augury,
  Sphinx of Uthuun, Epiphany), **partial/redirect prevention** (the en-Kor cluster + Deflecting Palm
  / Comeuppance / Channel Harm), **damage-doubling replacement** (Gisela, Bitter Feud),
  **exchange-control** (Legerdemain, Shifting Loyalties), **guess/hidden-number** (Master of
  Predicaments), and **"end the turn"** (Day's Undoing).
- **PZ1 (Planechase Anthology) contributes zero `type=Plane` cards** to its missing-list — every
  entry is an ordinary Commander card — so, as in the 2007–2009 era, there is **no OUT-OF-SCOPE
  planar bucket** here. The planar subsystem gap is real but doesn't surface in
  `missing_cards_by_sets/PZ1.txt`.
- The three "Masters" reprint products (MM2, TPR, EMA) behave as expected: their missing-lists are
  small and are entirely old-frame reprints of engine-blocked designs (en-Kor redirect, Precursor
  Golem copy-fan-out, Jetting Glasskite first-target counter) — no set-specific new mechanic.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). No card was moved to EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics proven
  elsewhere in the catalogue (Throwing Knife is the sole clean EASY).
