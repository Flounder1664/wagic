# Old-Era Coverage Audit — 2018–2020 (16 sets)

> **CORRECTION (2026-07-11):** this doc's treatment of **UND (Unsanctioned)** as wholesale
> out-of-scope is superseded. Un-sets are NOT out-of-scope — see
> [UNSETS_AUDIT.md](UNSETS_AUDIT.md). (UND turns out to be 100% reprints of the other three un-sets,
> so it adds no new designs, but its cards are reason-classified there.) Non-UND findings below stand.

Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**UMA · RNA · GK2 · WAR · MH1 · M20 · C19 · ELD · MB1 · THB · UND · C20 · IKO · M21 · 2XM · AKR**
(Ultimate Masters, Ravnica Allegiance, RNA Guild Kit, War of the Spark, Modern Horizons,
Core Set 2020, Commander 2019, Throne of Eldraine, Mystery Booster, Theros Beyond Death,
Unsanctioned, Commander 2020, Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths, Core Set 2021, Double Masters,
Amonkhet Remastered).

Generated 2026-07-10. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the SOS backlog. Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the
underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no supported example exists.
`PUMA.txt` (Ultimate Masters promos) is folded into UMA — its 4 cards are a strict subset of
UMA's list.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Splice (Arcane, or onto instant/sorcery)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

No primitive lets a card in hand be revealed at cast time to graft its effect onto another spell.
Every splice card in the catalogue is unsupported; the "Arcane" typing that splice keys off has
supported *hosts* (Kodama's Reach) but no supported *splice payload*.

- Goryo's Vengeance (UMA) — splice-Arcane; temp-reanimate a legendary
- Through the Breach (UMA) — splice-Arcane; put a creature from hand into play
- Desperate Ritual (UMA) — splice-Arcane; add {R}{R}{R}
- Everdream (MH1, MB1) — splice onto instant/sorcery; draw a card

*(Glacial Ray, the archetypal splice card, is likewise unsupported — confirming the family, not
just these instances, is engine-blocked. 4 distinct names here; the family is 5+ across sets.)*

### E2 — Vote — council's dilemma / will of the council  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

Multiplayer voting where each player picks a mode has no UI or resolution primitive. All probed
voters are unsupported.

- Council's Judgment (2XM) — will of the council: vote a nonland permanent, exile the winner
- Expropriate (MB1) — council's dilemma: time (extra turns) vs money (steal permanents)
- Lieutenants of the Guard (MB1) — council's dilemma: strength (counters) vs numbers (tokens)
- Messenger Jays (MB1) — council's dilemma: feather (counters) vs quill (loot)

*(4 distinct in these lists; the wider vote family — Tyrant's Choice, Split Decision — is equally
unsupported.)*

### E3 — Copy a spell "for each other target" (Precursor / Zada family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Single-target copy is supported (Twincast); "copy the spell for each other Golem it could target"
needs a copy-per-legal-target fan-out the engine has no analog for.

- Precursor Golem (MB1) — any instant/sorcery targeting one Golem is copied for each other Golem

### E4 — Copy every spell you cast (conspire / replicate / repeatable free-copy)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

A static that attaches a copy-rider to *all* of a player's spells (conspire tap-cost, replicate
mana-cost, or coin-flip loop). None supported.

- Wort, the Raidmother (C20) — your red/green instants & sorceries gain conspire
- Djinn Illuminatus (C20) — your instants & sorceries gain replicate
- Mirror March (RNA) — coin-flip-until-you-lose, copy the creature each win (token copies)

### E5 — ETB-trigger doubling  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"If a permanent entering causes a triggered ability to trigger, it triggers again" — Panharmonicon
class; both it and this card are unsupported.

- Yarok, the Desecrated (M20) — your permanents' ETB triggers fire an additional time

### E6 — Protection from a chosen / random converted mana cost  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Protection is keyed to color/name/type in the DSL; "protection from each CMC other than a chosen
number" (chosen at random or odd/even) has no numeric-CMC protection primitive.

- Haktos the Unscarred (THB) — choose 2/3/4 at random; protection from every other CMC
- Lavabrink Venturer (IKO) — choose odd or even; protection from each CMC of that parity

### E7 — "Has all activated abilities of…" (ability-absorption)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

Dynamically granting a permanent the full activated-ability set of other objects. Mairsil, the
canonical example, is also unsupported.

- Experiment Kraj (GK2) — has all activated abilities of each other +1/+1-countered creature
- Manascape Refractor (C20) — has all activated abilities of all lands in play
- (both fail identically on the live ability-copy engine)

### E8 — "End the turn" (turn truncation)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (3)

Exile-the-stack-and-skip-to-cleanup is a turn-structure operation the engine can't perform (Time
Stop / Sundial equally unsupported).

- Glorious End (AKR) — end the turn; you lose the game next end step
- Discontinuity (M21) — end the turn (cost-reduced on your turn)
- (Mandate of Peace's "end the combat phase" is a related turn-op — see E9)

### E9 — Reverse / truncate turn structure (other than "end the turn")  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Turn-order reversal and combat-phase termination — structural operations with no primitive.

- Aeon Engine (C19) — reverse the game's turn order
- Mandate of Peace (C19) — end the combat phase (remove attackers/blockers, exile the stack)

### E10 — Gain control of a spell on the stack  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No primitive to seize an object off the stack and redirect it (Commandeer/Aethersnatch both
unsupported).

- Sudden Substitution (C19) — split second; exchange control of a noncreature spell and a creature

### E11 — Exchange control of two permanents  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Two-way control swap of two chosen permanents. Legerdemain / Juxtapose both unsupported.

- Chromeshell Crab (C19) — face-up: exchange control of your creature and an opponent's

### E12 — Two-pile "opponent splits, you choose" (Fact-or-Fiction partition)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Opponent-partitions-a-revealed-set-into-two-piles has no partition UI (even Fact or Fiction itself
is only borderline; every reveal-into-two-piles card is unsupported).

- Atris, Oracle of Half-Truths (THB) — opponent splits your top 3 into face-up/face-down; you keep one pile

### E13 — Name a card from a revealed hand, then lock casting it  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Dynamic name-capture from a revealed hand plus a per-name cast-lock (Alhammarret family; both
unsupported). Distinct from the *pre-named* Nevermore/Meddling Mage, which are borderline.

- Isperia the Inscrutable (GK2) — combat damage: name a card, opponent reveals hand, tutor a flyer if hit

### E14 — Ping on any nonmana ability activation  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

A static that watches every opponent activated-ability activation and punishes it. Rhystic Circle
and the whole family are unsupported — there is no "on ability activated" global trigger hook.

- Burning-Tree Shaman (GK2) — 1 damage to a player whenever they activate a nonmana ability
- Harsh Mentor (AKR) — 2 damage to an opponent who activates a nonmana artifact/creature/land ability

### E15 — Splice-free reanimate/put-into-play with temp-exile rider  · ENGINE-BLOCKED-adjacent → see E1

(No separate group; Goryo's Vengeance and Through the Breach are filed under E1 at their hardest
blocker, the splice cost.)

### E16 — Manifest / face-down variant with a pile or move-source rider  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

Base manifest is supported (Rageform, Scroll of Fate is STALE-working); these move the manifest
*source* to a graveyard pile, another player's library, or bundle a turn-face-up rider the
primitive didn't author.

- Ghastly Conscription (C19) — exile a GY's creatures, shuffle, manifest the pile
- Write into Being (MB1) — look at top 2, manifest one
- Trail of Mystery (C19) — face-down ETB → land tutor; turned-face-up → +2/+2
- Thieving Amalgam (C19) — manifest the top of each opponent's library each upkeep + steal-death drain
- Kadena, Slinking Sorcerer (C19) — first face-down spell each turn costs {3} less; face-down ETB → draw

### E17 — Turn-face-up / morph rider on an otherwise-supported morph  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

Morph and the turn-face-up trigger are both supported; these carry a specific face-up rider the
primitive didn't author (counter-cast, GY recursion, exchange-control filed elsewhere).

- Kheru Spellsnatcher (C19) — face-up: counter a spell, exile it, may cast it free (also E10-adjacent)
- Deathmist Raptor (C19) — turn-face-up anywhere → return it from GY face up or down
- Gift of Doom (C19) — morph aura; face-up → attach; grants deathtouch + indestructible
- Vesuvan Shapeshifter (C19) — ETB/turn-face-up become a copy with a self-reflip clause
- (Chromeshell Crab's face-up is control-exchange → filed E11)

### E18 — Become-a-copy-of-a-target / copy-with-rider creature  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (4)

Base Clone is supported; these become a copy of a *chosen target* with extra riders (defender+tap,
constellation trigger, legendary-name retention) the primitive doesn't wire.

- Protean Thaumaturge (THB) — constellation: may become a copy of another target creature
- Wall of Stolen Identity (THB… C19 file) — enter as a copy + Wall + defender; tap-lock the copied creature
- Sakashima the Impostor (MB1) — enter as a copy keeping its own name + bounce ability
- Ixidron (C19) — ETB flip all other nontoken creatures face down; P/T = # face-down (dynamic P/T copy-adjacent)

### E19 — Council/vote-adjacent shared-target & tempting-offer  · ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM  (2)

- Tempt with Discovery (C19, MB1) — tempting offer: each opponent may also fetch a land, scaling your fetch · ENGINE-BLOCKED (tempting-offer, same missing multiplayer-choice machinery as votes)
- Pendant of Prosperity (C19) — enters under an opponent's control; symmetric draw/land-drop engine · MEDIUM (enter-under-opponent-control + shared upkeep draw)

### E20 — Give-control-away / gain-control riders  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Simple one-shot control gift works; these bundle an opponent-controlled toggle or a combat hand-off.

- Tahngarth, First Mate (C19) — when an opponent attacks, may hand them Tahngarth to attack for them
- Bloodthirsty Blade (C19) — equip that can attach to an *opponent's* creature + goad (goad itself works; opponent-attach is the gap)

### E21 — Loyalty-ability copy / re-activation  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Loyalty abilities themselves are supported; copying a loyalty activation on a coin/mana trigger is
not (The Chain Veil equally unsupported).

- Chandra's Regulator (M20) — copy a Chandra loyalty activation for {1}; discard-Mountain to draw

### E22 — Upkeep modal "choose one not yet chosen," incl. a lose-the-game mode  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Needs to remember which modes were chosen across turns and support the fourth "you lose."

- Demonic Pact (AKR) — each upkeep pick an unused mode; fourth is "you lose the game"

### E23 — Random-player exiles-and-recasts from a graveyard  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Choose-a-random-player + they exile a spell from GY + you copy/cast it free — the random-player
selection plus cross-owner exile-cast is the blocker.

- Wildfire Devils (C19) — ETB and each upkeep: random player exiles an instant/sorcery from GY; copy it free

### E24 — Play-from-a-special-exile-zone  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"You may play the cards exiled this way" — a per-card cross-zone cast permission the primitives
can't scope.

- Hedonist's Trove (C19) — exile an opponent's GY; play lands freely, one spell per turn from it
- Aminatou's Augury (MB1) — exile top 8; cast one card of each type from among them free

### E25 — Cost-reduce / X-scaling keyed on turn history or damage this turn  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Supported cost reducers are static; these read a running turn total (damage dealt, cards revealed)
the primitive can't accumulate.

- Chandra's Incinerator (M21) — costs {X} less = noncombat damage to opponents this turn; redirect noncombat damage
- Unbound Flourishing (MH1) — double every X you cast; copy X-cost spells/abilities · (borders ENGINE — the "copy any X ability" clause is copy-per-cast)
- Embodiment of Agonies (M20) — enters with +1/+1 per distinct mana cost among nonland GY cards

### E26 — Distribute keyword counters by graveyard survey (IKO keyword-counters)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Keyword counters exist (Lurking Deadeye borderline), but surveying the GY for each of 11 keywords
and stamping counters is a big multi-clause loop none of these author.

- Kathril, Aspect Warper (C20) — for each keyword present in your GY, put that keyword counter on a creature
- Selective Adaptation (C20) — reveal 7, pick one card of each of 11 keywords, distribute across zones

### E27 — Counter-move / counter-payoff engines  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Moving counters between permanents and re-homing them. `move counters` exists in isolation
(Slippery Bogbonder is STALE-working), but these bundle a combat-phase auto-move and a
leaves-battlefield capture.

- The Ozolith (IKO) — capture counters of creatures that leave; combat: move all onto a creature
- Tayam, Luminous Enigma (C20) — vigilance-counter anthem + remove-3-counters reanimator

### E28 — Reveal-until-shares-a-type / reveal-until-land count  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (3)

Reveal-from-top-until-a-condition then act on the count/card. Goblin Charbelcher (reveal-until-land)
and the shares-a-type diggers are all unsupported.

- Goblin Charbelcher (MB1) — reveal until a land, deal that many; double if Mountain
- Reality Scramble (MB1) — bottom a permanent, reveal-until-shares-a-type into play; retrace
- Unpredictable Cyclone (IKO) — replace cycling-draw with reveal-until-shares-a-type free cast

### E29 — Companion (deck-restriction-gated legend)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

The Companion keyword *is* supported (Lurrus, Yorion, et al. are borderline). These five are
excluded because their **body** (not the keyword) is unsupported — each pairs the deck-building
restriction with an ability the primitive didn't author (mill-and-reanimate, double-damage,
cost-reduction, five-color mana, can't-block).

- Gyruda, Doom of Depths (IKO) — even-CMC deck; ETB mill 4 each, reanimate an even creature
- Jegantha, the Wellspring (IKO) — no-doubled-symbol deck; taps for WUBRG
- Zirda, the Dawnwaker (IKO) — all-activated-ability deck; activated abilities cost {2} less
- Obosh, the Preypiercer (IKO) — odd-CMC deck; your odd sources deal double damage
- (deck-restriction validation + these bodies together = MEDIUM; keyword alone is trivial)

### E30 — Convoke + delve + cast-from-graveyard combo  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Convoke, delve, and cast-from-GY each exist; the "can't spend mana, pays entirely with
convoke+delve, castable from GY" stack is a careful multi-cost authoring job.

- Hogaak, Arisen Necropolis (MH1) — no mana; convoke + delve; castable from graveyard

### E31 — Dies → return transformed as an Aura on a creature  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Return-from-GY works; returning *as an Aura enchantment* attached to a creature, losing its other
abilities, is the gap (Accursed Witch was the same-family MEDIUM in the prior era).

- Bronzehide Lion (THB) — dies → return as an indestructible-granting Aura on a creature you control

### E32 — Static "can't cast noncreature spells CMC≥4 / with X"  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Name/type cast-locks work (Nether Void supported, Meddling Mage borderline); a *CMC-threshold*
noncreature lock plus an {X}-in-cost lock is a two-clause conditional restriction.

- Gaddock Teeg (UMA) — noncreature CMC≥4 can't be cast; noncreature with {X} can't be cast

### E33 — Protection-from-a-named-card / opponent-can't-make-you-sacrifice  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Runed Halo's "protection from the chosen name" needs the pre-named protection Nevermore-style hook
extended to *you as a player*; Sigarda's is a targeted anti-sacrifice static. Both unsupported.

- Runed Halo (UMA, M21) — name a card; you have protection from that name
- Sigarda, Host of Herons (UMA) — opponents' spells/abilities can't make you sacrifice

### E34 — Redirect all damage to one chosen permanent  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

Prevention exists only as flat Fog; "all damage to you and yours is dealt to one chosen permanent
instead," and the single-source-redirect, are the missing redirect machinery (same family as the
prior-era en-Kor cluster).

- Gideon's Sacrifice (WAR) — redirect all damage to you/your permanents onto one chosen permanent
- Kor Chant (MB1) — redirect a chosen source's damage from one of your creatures to another

### E35 — Grafdigger-style dual static (no ETB-from-GY/lib + no cast-from-GY/lib)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Leyline of the Void (GY-hate) is supported and Yixlid Jailer borderline, but the combined
"creatures can't ETB from GY/lib AND players can't cast from GY/lib" pair isn't authored.

- Grafdigger's Cage (M20) — the two-clause anti-recursion static

### E36 — Miscellaneous single-card riders on supported mechanics  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM / EASY  (mixed, 20)

Marquee mechanic supported; a specific rider blocks the card. Grouped to avoid a bucket per card.

- Biomancer's Familiar (RNA) — activated abilities of your creatures cost {2} less + adapt-reset · MEDIUM (cost-reduction static; adapt itself works)
- Azorius Guildmage (GK2) — tap-a-creature mode + counter-an-activated-ability mode · MEDIUM (counter-ability works; the dual-mode wiring is the gap)
- Watcher for Tomorrow (MH1) — hideaway; LTB → exiled card to hand · MEDIUM (hideaway works; the LTB retrieve is the rider)
- Valiant Changeling (MH1) — changeling + cost-reduction per creature-type-you-control · MEDIUM
- Mirrodin Besieged (MH1) — choose Mirran/Phyrexian; two very different bodies + a lose-condition · MEDIUM
- String of Disappearances (MH1) — bounce; controller may pay to copy-and-retarget the spell · MEDIUM (player-optional spell-copy chain)
- Thunderkin Awakener (M20) — attack: reanimate an Elemental tapped & attacking, temp · MEDIUM
- Divine Reckoning (C19) — each player keeps one creature, destroy the rest; flashback · MEDIUM (each-player-chooses board wipe)
- Thought Sponge (C19) — enter with +1/+1 = most cards an opponent drew this turn; dies → draw=power · MEDIUM (turn-history max)
- Marisi → STALE (borderline)
- Fervent Champion (ELD) — Knight lord on attack + self-targeting equip cost reduction · MEDIUM
- Rivals' Duel (MB1) — two creatures sharing no type fight · EASY (fight is supported; the "share no type" target constraint is minor)

## Confidence & caveats

- **100% of the 108 non-UND excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt`** — this audit measures
  the *documented* unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each
  set (everything Wagic *does* implement — the whole of ELD/THB/IKO/M20/M21 beyond these handful of
  entries) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope.
- **The era's marquee mechanics are overwhelmingly supported**: mutate, companion (keyword),
  devotion, escape, constellation, food, adamant, proliferate, amass, planeswalker-static,
  changeling, goad, morph/megamorph/manifest (base), clash, retrace, cumulative upkeep, and
  keyword-counters (base) all have supported/borderline exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is
  why most excluded cards land in **BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by a rider")** rather than
  ENGINE-BLOCKED — the headline mechanic works; a secondary clause doesn't.
- **Four cards are STALE and should be pulled from the missing-lists**: Secret Plans, Scroll of
  Fate, Marisi Breaker of the Coil (all C19), and Slippery Bogbonder (C20) — they resolve to
  `borderline`/`mtg.txt` and load today.
- **Genuinely engine-blocked patterns confirmed by absence of any supported example**: **splice**
  (no supported splice card exists — the single largest new engine-blocked family this era),
  **council's-dilemma / will-of-the-council voting** (multiplayer mode-voting), **copy-a-spell-for-
  each-target** (Precursor Golem), **copy-every-spell-you-cast** (conspire/replicate — Wort, Djinn
  Illuminatus, Mirror March), **ETB-trigger-doubling** (Yarok/Panharmonicon), **protection from a
  chosen/random CMC** (Haktos, Lavabrink), **ability-absorption** (Experiment Kraj, Manascape,
  Mairsil), **"end the turn"** (Glorious End, Discontinuity), **reverse-turn-order** (Aeon Engine),
  **gain-control-of-a-spell** (Sudden Substitution), **exchange-control** (Chromeshell Crab),
  **Fact-or-Fiction two-pile** (Atris), **name-from-a-revealed-hand cast-lock** (Isperia,
  Alhammarret), **ping-on-any-nonmana-ability-activation** (Burning-Tree Shaman, Harsh Mentor), and
  **retarget-a-spell** (Willbender, Spellskite — the "Willbender effect" from the prior-era doc,
  still unsupported).
- **UND (Unsanctioned) contributes 58 OUT-OF-SCOPE silver-border un-cards** — the entire file. This
  is the era's one large OUT-OF-SCOPE bucket (the analog of the "no Plane cards" note in prior
  eras, but inverted: here the whole set is out of scope). None are engine-fixable and none should
  be authored.
- **Reprint products behave as expected**: MB1 (Mystery Booster, 25 entries) and 2XM (4) are
  almost entirely old-frame reprints of designs already catalogued in earlier eras (Precursor
  Golem, Spellskite, Council's Judgment, Goblin Charbelcher, Reflector Mage, Coalition Honor Guard).
  UMA/PUMA overlap collapses cleanly. Only **three** names span two sets *within* this era
  (Everdream, Runed Halo, Tempt with Discovery).
- **Two hand-authored-but-unresolved cards**: the C19 file gives Willbender a full
  `autofaceup=…fizzle && activate castcard…` line and the C20 file gives Portal Mage a
  `phasealter` line, yet both names still resolve only to `unsupported.txt` in the index — the
  authored attempt was never promoted to a loading primitive. Flagged as unresolved, not working.
- **IKO companions are a keyword/body split worth noting for the backlog**: the Companion keyword
  itself loads (six borderline exemplars), so Gyruda/Jegantha/Zirda/Obosh are BACKLOG-MEDIUM (their
  deck-restriction check + unique body), not ENGINE-BLOCKED. A future pass could lift them cheaply
  by reusing the existing companion frame.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (Unbound Flourishing, Martyr's Cause, Goblin Game, Tempt with Discovery — noted inline).
  No card was moved to EASY unless its full text uses only mechanics proven elsewhere (Rivals' Duel
  and Strionic Resonator are the two clean-ish EASY candidates).
