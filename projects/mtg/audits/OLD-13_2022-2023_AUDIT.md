Cross-set audit of missing / unsupported cards for the release-order run
**NEC · NEO · NCC · CLB · 2X2 · DMC · BRC · BRR · J22 · DMR · ONC · ONE · SIR · SIS · MOC · MOM**
(Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty Commander, Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty, New Capenna Commander,
Commander Legends: Baldur's Gate, Double Masters 2022, Dominaria United Commander,
The Brothers' War Commander, The Brothers' War Retro Artifacts, Jumpstart 2022,
Dominaria Remastered, Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander, Phyrexia: All Will Be One,
Shadows over Innistrad Remastered, Shadows of the Past (SIR's companion insert set),
March of the Machine Commander, March of the Machine).

Generated 2026-07-06. Method per [`README.md`](README.md): each name from
`Res/missing_cards_by_sets/<CODE>.txt` was cross-checked against `grade_index.json`
(the union of the four `core.zip` primitive files). Classification is by **oracle text**, never
substring — same discipline as the prior era docs. Before any card was tagged ENGINE-BLOCKED, the
underlying mechanic was **probed** against the index to confirm no supported example exists.

silently work today. The remaining **139** are all catalogued-unsupported: upstream registered them
with full oracle text inside `sets/primitives/unsupported.txt` (grade *unsupported* → does not load,

`type=Phenomenon` cards. Unlike PZ1 (Planechase Anthology) in the 2014–2016 era, whose missing-list
contained **zero** planar cards, MOC's missing-list is **dominated** by them. These are all
OUT-OF-SCOPE for the same reason (no planar subsystem), but here the bucket is large.

## By exclusion reason (main deliverable)

Grouped by the mechanic/pattern that blocks each card, deduped, with per-card set tags. Tier tags:
**ENGINE-BLOCKED** (no Wagic analog) · **BACKLOG-EASY** · **BACKLOG-MEDIUM** · **OUT-OF-SCOPE**.

### E1 — Copy a triggered / activated ability you control  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (11)

The defining engine gap of 2022–2023. Every "copy target triggered/activated ability" (or "copy
that ability for the backup/attack trigger") is unsupported; the closest primitive, Lithoform
Engine, is borderline and its own file note reads `No copy for triggered abilities ATM`. This
pattern is spread across the era's commander products, the reprint masters sets, and MOC.

- Strionic Resonator (NCC, BRC, MOC) — {2},{T}: copy target triggered ability you control
- Weaver of Harmony (NEO) — {G},{T}: copy an activated/triggered ability from an enchantment source
- Aboleth Spawn (CLB) — copy an opponent's-ETB triggered ability
- Dynaheir, Invoker Adept (CLB) — copy the next ≥4-mana activated ability you use
- Wyll's Reversal (CLB) — d20 roll → may retarget and (15+) copy a spell/ability
- Grell Philosopher (CLB) — grant your Horrors all activated abilities of a target opponent's artifact
- Ashnod the Uncaring (BRC) — copy an artifact/creature ability if a permanent was sacrificed to it
- Agrus Kos, Eternal Soldier (J22) — copy an ability targeting only it, for each other creature (also E2 shape)
- Mirror-Shield Hoplite (MOM) — copy a **backup** ability that targeted a creature you control (backup itself works; the copy is the gap)
- Rowan's Talent (MOC) — copy the enchanted planeswalker's loyalty ability when activated

### E2 — Copy a spell "for each other legal target" (Precursor / Mirrorwing family)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Single-target copy is supported (Twincast/Fork), but "copy the spell once for each other Golem /
creature it could target, each copy a different one" needs a copy-per-legal-target fan-out the
engine has no analog for.

- Precursor Golem (BRR) — any spell targeting a single Golem is copied for each other Golem
- Mirrorwing Dragon (SIR) — a spell targeting only it is copied for each other creature you control

*(Agrus Kos, J22 — same fan-out shape on an ability; filed at E1 for its ability-copy blocker.)*

### E3 — "Triggers an additional time" (Panharmonicon effect)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (5)

"If [event] causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, it triggers an
additional time." No primitive intercepts and re-fires another permanent's trigger. Every probed
example (Panharmonicon, Yarok) is unsupported.

- Panharmonicon (2X2, J22) — artifact/creature ETB triggers fire twice
- Isshin, Two Heavens as One (NEO) — attack-triggers of your permanents fire twice
- Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines (ONE) — your ETB triggers fire twice; opponents' don't fire (also a replacement-suppression clause)
- Drivnod, Carnage Dominus (ONE) — creature-death triggers of your permanents fire twice

### E4 — Change the target of a spell or ability (Willbender effect)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

No primitive can retarget an object already on the stack (Spellskite unsupported; only the fixed
"Redirect" spell is even borderline).

- Spellskite (CLB) — {PU}: change a target of target spell or ability to Spellskite

### E5 — "Has all activated abilities of cards in a graveyard / all lands in graveyards"  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

A live, board-wide grant of *every* activated ability of cards in graveyards — the engine can't
enumerate-and-attach abilities from another zone dynamically (Necrotic Ooze unsupported).

- Necrotic Ooze (2X2) — has all activated abilities of all creature cards in all graveyards
- Mirran Safehouse (ONE) — has all activated abilities of all land cards in all graveyards

### E6 — Reveal-until-land, deal that many (Charbelcher)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Reveal-from-top-until-a-land, count the nonlands, deal that much (doubled on Mountain) — the
reveal-until-then-count loop has no primitive.

- Goblin Charbelcher (BRR) — reveal until a land; deal nonlands-revealed damage, double if Mountain

### E7 — Fact-or-Fiction two-pile (opponent partitions)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (2)

Reveal-N-then-split-into-two-piles-and-opponent-chooses has no partition UI (Fact or Fiction itself
is only borderline; every reveal-into-two-piles card here is unsupported).

- Gifts Ungiven (2X2) — tutor 4 different names; opponent picks 2 to your GY, rest to hand
- Epiphany at the Drownyard (SIR) — reveal top X+1, split into two piles, opponent chooses

### E8 — Ripple / cast copies from top of library  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

"Reveal the top N, cast the same-named cards free" — a reveal-and-cast-by-name loop.

- Thrumming Stone (2X2) — your spells gain ripple 4

### E9 — Cast / control an opponent's turn (Emrakul)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Gain control of an opponent *during their next turn* (Mindslaver-class turn hijack), plus a
graveyard-card-type cost reducer.

- Emrakul, the Promised End (SIR) — on cast, control target opponent's next turn

### E10 — Phase-out lock (choose-permanents, rest phase out, can't phase in)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED  (1)

Phasing itself exists (Vanishing borderline), but "all unchosen permanents phase out **and can't
phase in**" is a permanent-phase-lock the primitive can't express.

- Disciple of Caelus Nin (BRC) — each player keeps ≤5 permanents; the rest phase out permanently

### E11 — Improvise-granting static  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Improvise as a keyword works (Maverick Thopterist); *granting* improvise to all your nonartifact
spells (a cast-time cost-modification static) does not.

- Inspiring Statuary (BRR, MOC) — your nonartifact spells have improvise

### E12 — Experience-counter cost scaling  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Experience counters accrue fine (Ezuri borderline); the "{1} less per experience counter" cost
reduction on every I/S spell is the gap.

- Mizzix of the Izmagnus (2X2) — I/S spells cost {1} less per experience counter

### E13 — Copy-a-permanent on entry/attack (become a copy, keep counters)  · ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM  (3)

Base "enters as a copy" is supported (Clone/Body Double), but these copy on **attack** and return
the exiled original, exile-two-and-scale, or make a token copy of a target — all needing
copy-plus-bookkeeping the primitives don't wire up.

- Identity Thief (NCC) — attack: exile a creature, become a copy of it until EOT, return it later
- Stolen Identity (NCC) — token copy of target artifact/creature; plus **Cipher** (Hidden Strings unsupported)
- The Mimeoplasm (2X2) — ETB exile two GY creatures; become a copy of one +1/+1 = other's power

### E14 — Sacrifice-and-sum P/T on entry (Reign of the Pit / token = totals)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"Each player sacrifices; make an X/X where X = total power sacrificed." No sum-on-resolution
primitive.

- Reign of the Pit (NCC) — each player sacs a creature; X/X Demon where X = total power sacrificed
- Soul Separator (SIR) — split a GY creature into a 1/1 flying Spirit + an X/Y Zombie of its stats

### E15 — Counter-manipulation utility (move counters around)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Move a counter from one permanent to another as an activated ability — the counter-transfer
primitive is absent (Slippery Bogbonder, which does the same on ETB, is the *borderline* exemplar
and is STALE; Nesting Grounds' repeatable move is not implemented).

- Nesting Grounds (NCC) — {1},{T}: move a counter from one of your permanents to another

### E16 — Goad-on-attach / give-away equipment  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Goad itself works; "attach to a creature an opponent controls" plus the goad rider is the gap.

- Bloodthirsty Blade (NCC, CLB) — +2/+0, goaded; {1}: attach to a creature an opponent controls

### E17 — Combat-damage steal-then-cast (Thief of Sanity)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (1)

Exile the top 3 of a damaged player's library, keep one face-down, and grant permission to cast it
with any-color mana — a cross-zone cast-permission with a mana-color override.

- Thief of Sanity (NCC) — combat damage: peek 3, exile 1 face down, may cast it as any color

### E18 — CLB legendary commanders blocked by their body (not the Background line)  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (5)

"Choose a Background" and partner both work; these five fail on a *counter-replacement*,
counter-generating token engine, note-and-draw token, or opponent-searches trigger in their body.

- Volo, Itinerant Scholar (CLB) — creates a note-creature-types token, draw per noted type
- Lae'zel, Vlaakith's Champion (CLB) — counter-doubling **replacement** ("that many plus one")
- Raggadragga, Goreguts Boss (CLB) — mana-creature anthem + untap-on-attack + ≥7-mana payoff
- Jon Irenicus, Shattered One (CLB) — hand a creature to an opponent with counters + goad + can't-sac
- Archivist of Oghma (CLB) — flash; whenever an opponent searches their library, draw + gain life

### E19 — Opponent-search punisher / library-search lock  · BACKLOG-MEDIUM  (2)

"When an opponent searches their library, punish them" / "players can't search unless they pay."
Aven Mindcensor is borderline but these variants (sac + lose 10; global search tax) are unsupported.

- Ob Nixilis, Unshackled (2X2) — opponent search → sac a creature + lose 10 life
- Leonin Arbiter (2X2) — players can't search libraries unless they pay {2}

### PLANECHASE — `type=Plane` / `type=Phenomenon`  · OUT-OF-SCOPE  (45)

MOC's Planechase insert. Every one is registered in `unsupported.txt` and none loads — Wagic has

OUT-OF-SCOPE as a block, exactly like the planar gap noted (but not surfaced) in prior eras. This is
the single largest bucket of the era.

## Confidence & caveats

- 100% of the 139 excluded names resolve to `unsupported.txt` — this audit measures the *documented*
  unsupported set for the era, not raw Scryfall completeness. The bulk of each set (everything Wagic
  *does* implement) never appears in `missing_cards_by_sets` and is out of scope. **Zero truly-absent**
  (every missing name is catalogued with oracle text).
- The era's **marquee mechanics are overwhelmingly supported** and were probed to confirm it:
  **battle-type DFCs / Sieges** (MOM — *not* engine-blocked), backup, incubate/Phyrexian-transform,
  toxic/corrupted, convoke, channel/ninjutsu/reconfigure/saga (NEO), **initiative/dungeon/venture and
  Background/partner (CLB)**, goad, foretell, ward, flashback, encore, phasing, experience counters,
  and riot all have supported/borderline exemplars in `grade_index.json`. That is why most non-plane
  cards land in BACKLOG-MEDIUM ("blocked by a rider/payoff") rather than ENGINE-BLOCKED.
- **The signature engine gap of 2022–2023 is "copy a triggered or activated ability"** (Strionic
  Resonator, Rings of Brighthearth, Illusionist's Bracers, Weaver of Harmony, Ashnod, Rowan's Talent,
  Mirror-Shield Hoplite, …). It is confirmed by the file note on the closest primitive, Lithoform
  Engine: `#MISSING: No copy for triggered abilities ATM`. Its close cousin, the **Panharmonicon
  "triggers an additional time"** replacement, is equally unsupported across the era (Panharmonicon,
  Isshin, Elesh Norn, Drivnod, Yarok).
- **MOC dominates the era's missing-list and it is mostly Planechase.** 45 of 141 distinct names are
  `type=Plane`/`type=Phenomenon`, all OUT-OF-SCOPE for lack of a planar subsystem. This is the
  inverse of PZ1 (2014–2016), whose missing-list had **zero** planar cards — here the planar gap
  surfaces loudly. Filed as one OUT-OF-SCOPE block.
- **Two cards are STALE and should be pulled from the missing-lists**: Gimbal, Gremlin Prodigy (MOC)
  and Slippery Bogbonder (NCC) — both resolve to a loadable grade in `mtg.txt` and work today.
- The three reprint-heavy products behave as expected: **2X2** (Double Masters) and **DMR**
  (Dominaria Remastered) missing-lists are entirely old-frame reprints of engine-blocked designs
  (Panharmonicon, Necrotic Ooze, Thrumming Stone / Cryptic Gateway, Damping Sphere), and **J22**
  (Jumpstart 2022, a ~950-card grab-bag) contributes only a small, stale-free list of old riders —
  no set-specific new mechanic and no stale entries, despite its size.
- Tier assignment is a text-based judgment call; several cards sit on an ENGINE-BLOCKED / MEDIUM
  boundary (noted inline). **No card qualified as clean BACKLOG-EASY** this era — every non-plane
  excluded card carries a rider or payoff on top of its supported base mechanic.
