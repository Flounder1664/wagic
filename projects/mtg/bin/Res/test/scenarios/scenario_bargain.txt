#SCENARIO, not a test: hands control to you and asserts nothing.
#
#PHASE 2 SHORTFALL - bargain cannot eat a token.
#
#Real rule:  "You may sacrifice an artifact, ENCHANTMENT, OR TOKEN as you cast this spell."
#Wagic:       other={R}{S(artifact,enchantment|myBattlefield)} name(Bargain)
#             otherrestriction=type(*[artifact;enchantment]|mybattlefield)~morethan~0
#
#Only artifacts and enchantments count as fodder. Creature tokens do not, and because the
#restriction also gates the MENU, a board full of tokens means Bargain is not even offered.
#
#Reminder: Wagic never taps lands for you - tap Mountains yourself before each cast.
#
#RUN IT IN THIS ORDER, and watch the CAST MENU each time. That menu is the whole point.
#
#  1. Tap 2 Mountains, cast DRAGON FODDER.
#     -> two 1/1 Goblin TOKENS arrive. By the real rules these are now legal bargain fodder.
#
#  2. Tap 1 Mountain, cast TORCH THE TOWER at a Hill Giant.
#     -> *** THE SHORTFALL *** No "Bargain" entry appears, even though you control two
#        tokens you are entitled to sacrifice. You get the unbargained spell: 2 damage,
#        and the 3/3 Hill Giant SURVIVES.
#
#  3. Cast ORNITHOPTER (it costs {0}). It is an artifact creature.
#
#  4. Tap 1 Mountain, cast the second TORCH THE TOWER at the OTHER Hill Giant.
#     -> Now "Bargain" DOES appear, because an artifact is on the battlefield. Take it.
#        3 damage, and that Hill Giant DIES.
#
#The contrast between step 2 and step 4 is the bug: same board otherwise, same spell, and
#the only thing that changed is whether the fodder happened to be an artifact.
#
#Note it also eats your Ornithopter rather than a spare Goblin - with real bargain you
#would choose the token and keep the artifact.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:Dragon Fodder,Torch the Tower,Torch the Tower,Ornithopter
inplay:Mountain,Mountain,Mountain,Mountain,Mountain
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Hill Giant,Hill Giant
life:20
[DO]
human
[ASSERT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[END]
