#"<this> has all activated abilities of <selector>" - sixteen cards printed with it and not
#one implemented, because there was no way to grant an ability that another card owns.
#Both halves already existed: ATransformer parses a list of ability LINES onto a receiving
#card (that is what newability[...] is), and ListMaintainerAbility keeps a live set of cards
#matching a selector across every zone. allactivatedabilities(<selector>) joins them - the
#list of lines is read from each matching card's own magicText at runtime.
#
#Drana and Linvala borrows from creatures the OPPONENT controls. Give player 2 a Llanowar
#Elves and player 1 must be able to tap Drana and Linvala for {G}: one tapped permanent on
#player 1's side, none on player 2's, which is what proves the borrowed {T} taps the
#borrower and not the lender. Without the grant the click does nothing and the pool stays
#empty - and the pool has to be asserted in the same phase, because it empties on a change.
[INIT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Drana and Linvala
manapool:{0}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Llanowar Elves
life:20
[DO]
Drana and Linvala
[ASSERT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Drana and Linvala
tappedinplay:1
manapool:{G}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Llanowar Elves
tappedinplay:0
life:20
[END]
