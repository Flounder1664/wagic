#Airship Crash and Make Your Move both carried target(artifact,enchantment,creature[..]) -
#a comma-separated type list with a [..] restriction on one member. createTargetChooser
#returns NULL for that shape, and MTGAbility.cpp assigned tc->belongsToAbility OUTSIDE the
#NULL guard that sits right above it, so parsing either card SEGFAULTED the process. The
#guard was added for exactly this case and the assignment was appended after it.
#Found while checking whether borrowing a graveyard creature's lines could crash the game
#(wagic#42) - but it was never borrow-specific: casting either card did it.
#
#It cannot be written as one *[..]: CardDescriptor::mode is a single flag for the whole
#descriptor, overwritten term by term, so *[artifact;enchantment;creature&flying] ends up
#CD_AND and matches nothing. Rewritten as one gated choice per mode, the shape Deface uses.
#
#Make Your Move rather than Airship Crash: Airship Crash also carries an autohand cycling
#ability, and a click in hand reaches that instead of the cast, which is a harness artefact
#and not the thing under test. Both cards carry the identical broken selector.
#Choosing the power>=4 mode must destroy Serra Angel (4/4) and leave Grizzly Bears (2/2)
#alone - that is what shows the [power>=4] restriction survived the rewrite.
#It is "choice 0", not "choice 2", because the restriction{} clauses gate which modes are
#OFFERED: with no artifact and no enchantment on the board, the power>=4 mode is the only
#one on the menu. That the index moves is itself evidence the gating works.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:make your move
manapool:{2}{W}
[PLAYER2]
inplay:serra angel,grizzly bears
[DO]
make your move
goto firstmain
goto firstmain
choice 0
serra angel
goto secondmain
goto secondmain
[ASSERT]
SECONDMAIN
[PLAYER1]
graveyard:make your move
[PLAYER2]
inplay:grizzly bears
graveyard:serra angel
[END]
