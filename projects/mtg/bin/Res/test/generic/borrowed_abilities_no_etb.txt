#John, in game: Drana and Linvala offered a "create Goblin" option that came from an
#opponent's Goblin Gang Leader, whose token-making is an ETB and not an activated ability
#at all. She also could not be declared as an attacker or blocker while it was there, and
#both symptoms went away together once the opponent's creatures died.
#
#One cause. dynamic_cast<ActivatedAbility*> is NOT a test for "is an activated ability" -
#in this codebase ActivatedAbility means "can carry a cost", and plain resolution effects
#inherit it (ATokenCreator : public ActivatedAbility). So the ETB was granted as a costless
#entry in her ability menu, and a permanent with a spare {0} ability is exactly what makes
#clicking it open a menu instead of declaring it as an attacker.
#
#oneShot is the real discriminator: AbilityFactory::magicText resolves and deletes oneShot
#abilities and only addToGame()s the rest. Measured 2026-09-10 - Birds of Paradise
#{t}:add{w}, Goblin Sharpshooter {t}:damage:1, Skirk Prospector's {s} sacrifice cost and
#Benthic Biomancer's {1}{u} adapt are all oneShot=0; create(goblin...) is oneShot=1.
#
#Clicking Drana and Linvala with nothing but an ETB creature to borrow from must therefore
#do NOTHING. Before the fix it put two Goblins on HER controller's side, which is what the
#one-permanent assertion on player 1 catches.
#Player 2 holds three permanents, not one: seeding with inplay: fires Goblin Gang Leader's
#own ETB, which is correct and unrelated - the same thing Futurist Forge's test ran into.
[INIT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Drana and Linvala
manapool:{0}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Goblin Gang Leader
life:20
[DO]
Drana and Linvala
[ASSERT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Drana and Linvala
tappedinplay:0
manapool:{0}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
inplay:*,*,*
life:20
[END]
