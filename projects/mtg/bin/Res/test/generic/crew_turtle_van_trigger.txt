#Turtle Van: "put a +1/+1 counter on target creature that crewed it this turn."
#This is the card in John's deck60, and it was inert - not because the trigger was wrong but
#because the crewed counter was stamped with a capital C from C++ while every auto= line is
#lowercased on load, so the filter matched nothing. 3/3 means the whole chain works: crew
#stamps, the filter finds the crewer, the trigger resolves on it.
#Player 2 on 16 proves the 4/4 Vehicle actually attacked, so a 2/2 Grizzly Bears would mean
#the trigger, not a Vehicle that never got into combat.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Turtle Van,Grizzly Bears
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[DO]
Turtle Van
Grizzly Bears
next
#combat begins
next
#attackers
Turtle Van
next
#blockers
next
#damage
next
next
#combat end - the attack trigger eats a beat, hence the extra step
[ASSERT]
COMBATEND
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Turtle Van,Grizzly Bears
pt:Grizzly Bears=3/3
counter:Grizzly Bears=Crewed:1
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:16
[END]
