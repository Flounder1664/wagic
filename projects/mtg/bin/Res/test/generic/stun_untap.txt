#Stun (CR 122.1d): "if a permanent with a stun counter on it would become untapped, instead
#remove a stun counter from it." The engine had no stun at all - the counter was a name with no
#effect - so every stun card stunned nothing. Found playtesting Shackle Slinger.
#Icy Manipulator taps player 2's Grizzly Bears, Cryogen Relic stuns them, and at player 2's next
#untap step the Bears must STAY tapped with the stun counter gone. (Cryogen Relic draws on entering
#and on dying, hence two cards in player 1's hand.)
[INIT]
firstmain
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Icy Manipulator,Cryogen Relic
library:Forest,Forest,Forest
manapool:{2}{U}
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Grizzly Bears
[DO]
Icy Manipulator
Grizzly Bears
Cryogen Relic
Grizzly Bears
eot
next
[ASSERT]
UPKEEP
[PLAYER1]
inplay:Icy Manipulator
graveyard:Cryogen Relic
hand:*,*
library:*
tappedinplay:1
[PLAYER2]
inplay:Grizzly Bears
tappedinplay:1
counter:grizzly bears=stun:0
[END]
