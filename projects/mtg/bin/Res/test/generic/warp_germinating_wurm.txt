#WARP on a card that also has an ETB trigger. Germinating Wurm costs {4}{G} with
#Warp {1}{G} and gains 2 life on entry.
#This is the case that could break silently: adventure cards taught us that a bare
#auto= ETB line placed AFTER an "alternative" line misparses, so the warp line is
#appended after the existing autos rather than next to other=. If that ordering
#were wrong the life gain would go missing while everything still looked fine.
#Pool is exactly {1}{G}, so only the warp cast is affordable. Asserts all three:
#it resolved (life 22), and it exiled itself at the next end step.
[INIT]
FIRSTMAIN
[PLAYER1]
hand:Germinating Wurm
manapool:{1}{G}
life:20
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[DO]
Germinating Wurm
choice 1
eot
[ASSERT]
UNTAP
[PLAYER1]
exile:Germinating Wurm
life:22
[PLAYER2]
life:20
[END]
